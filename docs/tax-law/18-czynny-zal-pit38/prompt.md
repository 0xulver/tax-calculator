# External Research Prompt: Czynny Zal For PIT-38 Correction And Late Filing

## Objective

We need careful external legal research on Polish `czynny zal` / active regret
for a concrete PIT-38 cleanup. The taxpayer is concerned about possible
penal-fiscal consequences and wants to know what to file, what the active regret
can protect against, and how much factual explanation to include.

Treat this as adversarial legal research. If the proposed active-regret approach
is unnecessary, incomplete, risky, or worded badly, say so clearly.

## External Agent Instructions

Do not assume access to any local repository, private evidence, CSVs, prior
reports, or markdown files. Work only from the facts in this prompt and from
your own legal research.

Use current primary and high-authority sources wherever possible:

- Kodeks karny skarbowy, especially art. 9, 10, 16, 16a, 54, 56, 57 and any
  relevant limitation/fine provisions;
- Ordynacja podatkowa, especially art. 81 and 81b on corrections;
- PIT Act provisions and official PIT-38 guidance;
- Ministry of Finance / KAS / podatki.gov.pl guidance on e-czynny zal,
  corrections, PIT-38, crypto reporting, e-Urzad, and jurisdiction;
- court judgments or professional commentary only as secondary support;
- practical KAS/e-Urzad instructions if they affect filing sequence.

Clearly separate:

- black-letter law,
- official administrative guidance,
- case law / commentary,
- inference,
- practical recommendation.

Please cite sources with links, article numbers, and dates. If a rule differed
between the date of the offence and today's law, explain which version applies
and whether any more-favorable-law principle matters.

## Preliminary Understanding To Verify

The current working view is:

- 2024 missing return is primarily an art. 16 `czynny zal` problem if no return

## Research Questions

### A. Scope and effect of czynny zal

1. What exactly does KKS art. 16 protect against?
2. Does an effective `czynny zal` eliminate penal-fiscal punishability entirely,
   or merely mitigate punishment?
3. What conditions must be met for effectiveness, including timing, disclosure
   of material circumstances, cooperating persons, and payment of any public-law
   receivable?
4. What does it not protect against? For example: tax liability, interest,
   wrong tax merits, later audit adjustments, civil/procedural consequences.
5. Can `czynny zal` be submitted electronically through e-Urzad Skarbowy today?
   What signing/authentication requirements apply?
6. What is the proper authority/office for submitting it for individual PIT-38:
   current residence office, office for the year concerned, or another office?

### B. 2023 wrong filed PIT-38

7. For a PIT-38 already filed for 2023 but materially wrong, is KKS art. 16a
   sufficient if the taxpayer files a legally effective correction?
8. Does art. 16a apply where the correction changes cost/carry-forward fields
   but tax due for that year remains `0 PLN`?
9. Does art. 16a protect only against offences "concerning filing a declaration",
   or could some separate offence remain uncovered?
10. Is there any practical benefit or risk in also mentioning the 2023 correction
    in an art. 16 `czynny zal`?
11. If the 2023 wrong return reduced tax due only in later years by affecting
    carry-forward costs, how is "narażenie na uszczuplenie" assessed?

### C. 2024 missing or auto-accepted PIT-38

12. If no 2024 PIT-38 exists, is the correct remediation a late first filing
    rather than a correction?
13. Which KKS provisions are the most relevant for a late PIT-38 that ultimately
    shows `0 PLN` tax due?
14. Does KKS art. 54 require exposure of tax to underpayment? If tax due is zero,
    is art. 54 inapplicable or still arguable?
15. Does KKS art. 56 §4 or another provision punish late filing even without tax
    underpayment? If yes, what is the penalty range and limitation period?
16. If Twoj e-PIT auto-accepted a zero PIT-38 for 2024, is the taxpayer in an
    art. 16a correction scenario instead of an art. 16 active-regret scenario?
17. Can a single `czynny zal` safely cover both:
    - the 2023 correction, and
    - the 2024 late filing/correction?

### D. "Tax company" explanation and material circumstances

18. Should the taxpayer mention that they used a tax company and told the company
    they worked in crypto?
19. Does mentioning the tax company create any obligation to identify that
    company or its staff as "persons cooperating" in the offence?
20. Under KKS art. 9 §3 or related rules, can a professional tax preparer become
    liable as a person handling another person's financial/tax affairs? If yes,
    does that affect what the taxpayer should say in active regret?
21. Is it better to say:
    - "I relied on professional tax assistance and the PIT-38 crypto history was
      not reconstructed," or
    - "the tax company forgot / failed to ask / was busy"?
22. Can including reliance on professional assistance help show lack of intent,
    justified mistake, or lower culpability? Could it hurt by implying the
    taxpayer knew crypto was relevant but did not provide enough data?
23. Should the active regret explicitly say the taxpayer did not understand the
    Polish PIT-38 rules after moving to Poland, or is that unnecessary?
24. Does KKS art. 10 on justified mistake / lack of awareness of punishability
    matter here, or should the filing rely only on art. 16?

### E. Timing and authority knowledge

25. What makes `czynny zal` ineffective because the authority already had
    "clearly documented knowledge" or started official actions?
26. Does the mere absence of a 2024 PIT-38 in KAS systems count as documented
    knowledge of the offence?
27. Does a wrong 2023 PIT-38 already filed in the system count as documented
    knowledge of the 2023 offence?
28. Does automatic exchange of information, exchange reporting, PIT-8C, DAC8, or
    other crypto data in government systems affect effectiveness?
29. Should the taxpayer avoid calling or visiting the tax office before filing

### F. Wording and practical filing sequence

30. What should the active-regret notice contain in this exact case?
31. Should it use words like "czyn zabroniony", "nieprawidlowe wykonanie
    obowiazkow", "niezlozenie w terminie", "korekta", "zalegle zeznanie"?
32. Should it explicitly state that PIT-38 tax due is `0 PLN` for 2023 and 2024?
33. Should it promise payment if the authority determines any public-law amount

34. Should it attach or reference the PIT-38 corrections/late filing?
35. What order is safest:
    - submit `czynny zal`, then immediately file 2023/2024 PIT-38;
    - file PIT-38 first, then `czynny zal`;
    - file everything in the same e-Urzad session?
36. Should 2025 PIT-38 be mentioned at all, given it is not late?
37. What UPO/confirmation should be retained?

### G. If the zero-tax position is later challenged

38. If the tax office later disagrees with the PIT-38 cost basis and assesses

    punishment for the disclosed late/wrong filing?
39. Would additional `czynny zal`, correction, or payment be needed if a later
    correction changes tax from `0 PLN` to a positive amount?
40. Should a taxpayer in this situation consult a doradca podatkowy, adwokat, or
