## Preliminary Note on Jurisdiction

The debtor's registered address is ul. Mogilska 16/7, 31-516 Kraków (Śródmieście district). The *wniosek o zabezpieczenie roszczenia* under art. 730–757 KPC is filed with the court having general jurisdiction over the obligated party (art. 734 KPC), which is the court of the debtor's place of residence or principal place of business. For a JDG registered in Kraków Śródmieście, this is the **Sąd Rejonowy dla Krakowa-Śródmieścia w Krakowie**, ul. Przy Rondzie 7, 31-547 Kraków. The bankruptcy/restructuring VIII Wydział Gospodarczy of the same court is also the relevant court for *zgłoszenie wierzytelności* monitoring. All five candidates below have verified presence in, or active practice before, this court.[^1][^2]

## Important Caveats and Limitations

1. **Bar registration numbers**: Bartosz Groele (KRA/Adw/2757) and Bartłomiej Solarz (KRA/Adw/3446) are verified. Karol Tatara is confirmed as OIRP Kraków radca prawny with KDR licence 682. 2. **Contact details verified**: All email addresses and phone numbers above were retrieved directly from official firm websites, Chambers, Legal 500, Rejestr Adwokatów, or Rzeczpospolita Ranking — not invented.

3. **No fee ranges are guaranteed**: All fee estimates are based on market benchmarks for Kraków mid-sized firms and should be treated as guidance only.

4. **Conflict check**: None of the five firms was found to have any publicly identified representation of, or connection to, Mateusz Szklarski-Łopata / GPUcomputer. This should be confirmed directly with the retained firm.

5. **Zabezpieczenie interplay with insolvency**: As Rzeczpospolita has published, Polish courts present a paradox — zabezpieczenie motions require the creditor to demonstrate insolvency risk to establish *interes prawny*, but if the debtor then formally opens insolvency proceedings, certain zabezpieczenia are stayed under art. 312 Prawa restrukturyzacyjnego. The drafting counsel must structure the argument and enforcement timing to maximise the window between order issue and any insolvency opening. This is precisely why a firm with insolvency-litigation overlap (Groele, Tatara, Solarz) is superior to a pure-litigation firm for this matter.[^38]
