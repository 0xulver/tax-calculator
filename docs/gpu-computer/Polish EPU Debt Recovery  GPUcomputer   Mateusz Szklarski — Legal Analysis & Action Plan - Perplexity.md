## 1. Personal Liability of a JDG Proprietor (Question 1 & 2)

### Unlimited Personal Liability

A JDG is not a separate legal entity. In Polish law, a sole proprietor is identical to the natural person who operates it. There is **no corporate veil**, no share capital requirement, and no statutory liability cap. The entrepreneur is personally and jointly liable for all business debts with the entirety of their personal and business assets — real property, bank accounts, vehicles, receivables, and future earnings.[^4][^5][^6][^7]

Critically, Polish law does not apply the principle of subsidiarity to JDG creditors. This means a creditor **does not need to exhaust business assets first**; enforcement can be directed at personal assets immediately and simultaneously with business assets.[^7]

### Enforcement Survives Business Closure

If Szklarski closes or suspends GPUcomputer in the CEIDG register, the underlying debt does not vanish. A judgment obtained against "Mateusz Szklarski trading as GPUcomputer" is enforceable against him personally. Even after deregistration from CEIDG, any outstanding obligations remain his personal debt. A bailiff (*komornik*) can seize personal bank accounts, salary (up to 50% of net), real estate, movable property, and other assets.[^8][^9]

### Applicable Legal Provisions

- **Art. 551 KC** (Civil Code) — definition of an enterprise as belonging to the entrepreneur as a natural person
- **Art. 22 Ustawa o swobodzie działalności gospodarczej / Prawo przedsiębiorców** — JDG operates in the entrepreneur's own name and on their own account
- **Art. 776–795 KPC** — general enforcement provisions applicable against the entrepreneur personally

## 2. Current EPU Status: Is One Month of No Movement Normal? (Question 5)

### EPU Processing Times

The EPU (Sąd Rejonowy Lublin-Zachód) is the single dedicated e-court for all payment order claims in Poland. Processing times vary significantly depending on caseload. In favourable conditions a *nakaz zapłaty* can issue in 48 hours to a few weeks. Average time from filing to payment order is approximately 2–3 months, though spikes in filings can extend this. The status **"Wpływ" / "Zadekretowano – Nowa"** simply means the case has been received and assigned/docketed — it is the standard initial state, not a sign of any problem.[^10][^11]

**One month with no ruling is within normal bounds.** The EPU court issues orders by batch, and the referendarz (court officer) reviewing the case has up to the statutory general deadline. There is no legal reason for concern yet; the case has not been transferred, dismissed, or rejected.

### When to Act

If no nakaz zapłaty (payment order) has been issued by **~10 June 2026** (approx. 2 months from filing), it is reasonable to:
1. Call the EPU helpline (+48 81 534 10 10 or via the e-sad.gov.pl contact section) to confirm no technical issue

## 3. The Bankruptcy/Restructuring Risk (Questions 3, 4, 8)

### What the Debtor's Message Means

The seller stated he has "filed for bankruptcy protection" (*złożył wniosek o ochronę/restrukturyzację*). Under Polish law, there are four types of restructuring proceedings under the *Prawo restrukturyzacyjne* (Restructuring Law of 15 May 2015):[^12]

| Proceeding | Polish Name | Key Feature |
|---|---|---|
| Arrangement approval | *Postępowanie o zatwierdzenie układu* | Initiated by debtor; minimal court involvement at start |
| Accelerated arrangement | *Przyspieszone postępowanie układowe* | Court-opened; moratorium on enforcement |
| Standard arrangement | *Postępowanie układowe* | Full court process; moratorium |
| Remedial (sanacja) | *Postępowanie sanacyjne* | Deepest restructuring; strongest protection |

For a JDG, the debtor could alternatively apply for personal/business bankruptcy (*upadłość*) under the *Prawo upadłościowe* (Bankruptcy Law of 28 February 2003). Since March 2020, a JDG proprietor can also avail themselves of simplified consumer-style bankruptcy by first deregistering from CEIDG.[^13]

### Does No KRZ Result Mean No Proceeding Has Opened?

**Yes, with high confidence.** Every opening of a restructuring or bankruptcy proceeding in Poland must be publicly announced in the KRZ. The KRZ is the sole mandatory register for such announcements. The KRZ is updated by courts, not debtors — so a debtor cannot "hide" a formally opened proceeding.[^14][^15][^16]

**Important caveat:** Filing a *petition* for restructuring or bankruptcy is not the same as the court *opening* proceedings. It is possible the debtor has filed a petition (wniosek) that the court has not yet accepted — in which case the KRZ would show nothing. The debtor may also be exaggerating or mistaken about the stage of proceedings. Continue monitoring KRZ (krz.ms.gov.pl) using NIP 8661681248, the name "Szklarski," and "GPUcomputer" — **check at minimum every 2–3 days**.

### Also Check CEIDG

Search ceidg.gov.pl by NIP 8661681248 to verify whether the business is still listed as "Aktywna," "Zawieszona," or "Wykreślona". A suspension or deregistration ahead of a bankruptcy filing would be a warning sign and could affect enforcement strategy.[^9][^17]

## 4. Effect of Bankruptcy/Restructuring on the EPU Claim (Question 4)

### If Bankruptcy Is Declared (Ogłoszenie upadłości)

Under Art. 144 *Prawo upadłościowe*, once bankruptcy is formally declared:
- All pending civil proceedings touching the bankruptcy estate are **automatically suspended** by operation of law (*z mocy prawa*), without any court decision needed[^18][^19]
- Under Art. 146 PU, any enforcement proceedings (*egzekucja*) already underway are also suspended, and upon the order becoming final are **dismissed**[^20][^21]
- New enforcement from the bankruptcy estate becomes prohibited[^20]

The EPU proceeding would be suspended. **The EPU itself would not be automatically dismissed** — it suspends and you must switch strategy to filing a *zgłoszenie wierzytelności* (proof of claim) with the bankruptcy trustee (*syndyk*).

### If Restructuring Is Opened

The effect depends on the type of restructuring. In most types, enforcement is stayed but civil proceedings may continue. In *postępowanie sanacyjne* (remedial proceedings), there is an automatic moratorium (*zakaz egzekucji*) during the proceeding.[^12]

Under the August 2025 amendment implementing the EU Second Chance Directive, creditors now have enhanced voting rights and access to a creditor satisfaction test to evaluate arrangement proposals.[^22]

### Filing a Proof of Claim (*Zgłoszenie wierzytelności*)

If bankruptcy is declared, you must file a *zgłoszenie wierzytelności* as follows:[^23][^24][^25]

1. **Deadline:** 30 days from the date of the KRZ announcement (*obwieszczenie*) of bankruptcy — this is a strict statutory deadline under Art. 491(5) PU[^24]
2. **Where to file:** Via the KRZ system (online at krz.ms.gov.pl) — all bankruptcy filings are now fully electronic[^26]
3. **Form:** Mandatory official form in the KRZ system
4. **Content:** Full name and address; NIP; precise claim amount (155,000 PLN principal + accrued interest); legal basis (refund agreement confirmed by email, 10 March 2026); all evidence (proforma invoice, emails, postal proof); category of claim (see §5 below)[^23]
5. **Cost:** Filing within the 30-day deadline is **free of charge**. Late filings attract procedural costs[^25]

You can still file after the deadline until the proceeding closes, but late filing costs are borne by the creditor.[^24][^25]

## 5. Creditor Priority Categories in Polish Bankruptcy (Question 7 context)

Under Art. 342 *Prawo upadłościowe*, claims are satisfied in four categories:[^27]

| Category | What's included | Recovery prospect |
|---|---|---|
| I | Costs of proceedings, employees' wages (3-month cap), certain maintenance | High — paid first |
| II | ZUS/tax arrears (up to 1 year), other employee claims | Moderate |
| III | **Ordinary commercial creditors** (your claim fits here) | Depends on estate size |
| IV | Interest accrued after bankruptcy declaration, gifts, etc. | Very low |

Your claim (commercial sale of goods/refund of payment) falls in **Category III** — the general unsecured creditor pool. In practice, if there are significant ZUS/tax arrears or mortgage-secured creditors, Category III creditors may receive only cents on the złoty. For a small JDG with supplier exposure, recovery from a formal bankruptcy estate is uncertain and often partial.

## 6. Zabezpieczenie Roszczenia — Asset Freeze Motion (Question 9)

### What It Is and Why It Matters Now

*Zabezpieczenie roszczenia* (interim protection / precautionary attachment) under Art. 730–757 KPC is the most powerful tool available to you **right now**, before the nakaz zapłaty is issued or becomes enforceable. It allows a court to **freeze the debtor's assets preemptively** so they cannot be dissipated before judgment and enforcement.[^28][^29][^30]

Under Art. 730¹ §1 KPC, you must demonstrate two things:[^30]
1. **Uprawdopodobnienie roszczenia** — make the claim credible (*uprawdopodobnione*, lower threshold than full proof): your email thread, proforma invoice, refund confirmation, and pre-court demand are more than sufficient
2. **Interes prawny** — legal interest: the debtor's own statement about bankruptcy proceedings and failure to pay despite a written promise is strong evidence that non-action would make enforcement impossible or seriously difficult[^31]

### Available Attachment Methods (Art. 747 KPC)[^32][^33]

- **Bank account freeze** (*zajęcie wierzytelności z rachunków bankowych*) — most effective; can specify known account (PL84 2490 0005 0000 4530 9127 6540 per public records) or all accounts[^34]
- **Forced mortgage** (*hipoteka przymusowa*) on real property owned by Szklarski personally
- **Seizure of movables and vehicles**
- **Receivables and other property rights**

### Where to File

The *wniosek o zabezpieczenie* should be filed **separately from the EPU** — it cannot currently be filed within the EPU system. File at the **Sąd Rejonowy właściwy dla pozwanego** (General Jurisdiction District Court for Kraków, since the debtor is at ul. Mogilska 16/7, Kraków). The application must set out the claim, its legal basis, the amount (155,000 PLN + interest), the requested method of attachment, and proof of legal interest.

**Filing fee:** 100 PLN (fixed fee for precautionary attachment application in civil cases).

**Important timing:** If bankruptcy is formally declared *before* the attachment order becomes enforceable, it cannot be executed. File this application **as soon as possible** — days matter.[^20]

## 7. Criminal Fraud Complaint — Art. 286 KK (Question 9)

### Fraud vs. Contract Breach: The Key Distinction

Under Art. 286 §1 *Kodeks Karny*, criminal fraud (*oszustwo*) requires proof that the perpetrator **intentionally deceived** the victim at the time of contracting or at some material point, with the specific purpose of obtaining an unlawful financial benefit. The penalty is 6 months to 8 years imprisonment.[^35][^36]

A mere failure to deliver — even with repeated delays — is not automatically fraud. Courts require evidence of the *dolus directus* (direct intent): that the seller knew from the outset (or at a critical point) that delivery was impossible or that repayment would not occur.[^37][^35]

### Assessment of Your Case

Your facts present a **mixed picture**:
- **Factors supporting fraud argument:** Extended delays after receiving full payment, a written refund promise that was immediately broken, claiming bankruptcy protection while offering unclear timelines, possible pattern of similar behavior with other customers
- **Factors against fraud:** The seller has an established business since 2015, has cited a verifiable external cause (Hong Kong supplier bankruptcy), and has at least communicated throughout the process[^38][^1]

### Recommendation on Criminal Complaint

Filing a *zawiadomienie o podejrzeniu popełnienia przestępstwa* (criminal complaint) under Art. 304 §1 KPK at the nearest *Prokuratura Rejonowa* or *Komisariat Policji* is worth considering as a **parallel pressure tool**, with realistic expectations:[^39]

- It is **free** and can be withdrawn
- It imposes psychological pressure on the debtor to settle
- If the prosecutor opens an investigation, they have coercive tools (bank account freezes, asset searches) that a civil court lacks
- If the complaint is rejected (*umorzenie*), it costs nothing; you lose no civil rights

However, do not expect a criminal complaint to accelerate money recovery — criminal proceedings are slow (12–36+ months), and Polish courts rarely convict for commercial non-delivery absent clear proof of intent to deceive from the start.[^35]
