This is not a hypothetical question. The client needs to retain counsel within ~7 days. Treat this as a real procurement task: give me names I can actually contact, not a generic explainer of how to find a lawyer in Poland.

## Selection criteria — what makes a good fit

Rank lawyers/firms on these criteria; the top candidates should score well on most of them:
