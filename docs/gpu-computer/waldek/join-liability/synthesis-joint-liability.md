## 1. Strong consensus across all four sources

### 1.1 EPU subjective amendment is procedurally impossible

All four agree. Adding Waldemar to Nc-e 552126/26 via the e-court is not a
viable path. The e-court's teleinformatic system has no input pathway for
mid-case party addition; art. 505²⁸ KPC and the post-2020 EPU architecture
treat the procedure as a one-shot, document-light, single-defendant filter.
A motion to add Waldek through the EPU portal will either be rejected
outright or, on the most plaintiff-favourable reading, trigger
discontinuance (*umorzenie*) — not transfer to ordinary procedure.

### 1.2 The post-2020 reform replaced "transfer" with "discontinuance"

All four cite the 2019 reform (Ustawa z dnia 4 lipca 2019 r., Dz.U. 2019
poz. 1469), with EPU points entering into force **7 February 2020**.
Articles 505³³ and 505³⁶ KPC now provide *umarza postępowanie*
(discontinues proceedings) where formerly they provided *przekazuje sprawę
do sądu właściwości ogólnej* (transfers to court of general jurisdiction).
The brief I authored before the research outputs was based on pre-2020
commentary and is corrected on this point.

### 1.3 EPU Nc-e 552126/26 is structurally fragile because of Mateusz's NL residence

All four flag art. 505²⁸ § 2(2) KPC: a *nakaz zapłaty* cannot issue in EPU
if service would occur outside Poland. Mateusz lives in Eindhoven. Two of
the four (Perplexity Computer, Gemini) treat this as making discontinuance
**mandatory** once the e-court attempts service; Perplexity Pro treats it
as a fragility; ChatGPT acknowledges it as a real risk. Gemini reads the
discontinuance as already-inevitable and builds the entire procedural plan
around it. **The Polish virtual office at Mogilska 16/7 is the only ground
on which the EPU could survive at all**, and that depends on whether the
e-court accepts a virtual-office address for service purposes — which is
contested.

This is the single most consequential finding of the research run, beyond
the Waldek question itself. The EPU was always more fragile than the
filing date implied.

### 1.4 Art. 505³⁷ § 2 KPC preserves the filing-date — but only for the same claim against the same defendant

Unanimous reading. If the e-court discontinues and the plaintiff refiles
*the same claim against the same defendant* in ordinary procedure within
**3 months from the date of the discontinuance order** (not service —
issuance), the legal effects of bringing the action backdate to the
2026-04-08 EPU filing date. This preserves limitation interruption,
interest accrual, and lis pendens **as to Mateusz only**. Waldek, as a new
defendant, receives a new filing date regardless of how the suit is
structured. Gemini's headline "unified suit preserves priority" is
correct only with this caveat.

### 1.7 Art. 422 KC (joint tortfeasor / aider / beneficiary) is the strongest near-term theory

All four converge on this. Specifically:

- Polish text: *"Za szkodę odpowiedzialny jest nie tylko ten, kto ją
  bezpośrednio wyrządził, lecz także ten, kto inną osobę do wyrządzenia
  szkody nakłonił albo był jej pomocny, jak również ten, kto świadomie
  skorzystał z wyrządzonej drugiemu szkody."*
- Combined with art. 441 § 1 KC → solidary liability of co-tortfeasors.
- Combined with art. 415 KC → underlying fault-based tort of civil fraud
  (concealment of inability to perform, mapping onto the SN V KK 240/17
  criminal-fraud framing analogically applied civilly).

**The key SN ruling** identified by Perplexity Pro and Gemini is
**SN II CSKP 607/23 (3 July 2025)**: art. 422 KC liability attaches to
helpers acting with *dolus eventualis*; strict but-for causation is not
required — it is sufficient that the helper's conduct *facilitated* the
tort. This 2025 ruling materially lowers the evidentiary bar against
Waldek compared to pre-2025 commentary.

Cross-checked authorities: SN III CZP 89/17 (knowing benefit requires
actual knowledge, not constructive), SN III CSK 338/06 (art. 422 liability
is self-contained — plaintiff need not first sue or judge the direct
perpetrator), SA Gdańsk I ACa 39/15 (helper's "creating conditions for the
harm" is sufficient causal contribution).

### 1.8 De facto spółka cywilna (art. 860 + art. 864 KC) is a fallback, not a lead theory

Consensus that the *shared website + shared mobile + shared brand for a
decade* facts support an implied-partnership pleading, but that Polish
courts are wary of inferring spółka cywilna where both parties maintained
separate active CEIDG entries with separate NIPs. The 2008 (Waldek) vs.
2015 (Mateusz) registration sequence is itself evidence that the parties
intended formal separation, not a partnership. Three of the four put the
acceptance probability in the 20–50 % range; only with developed evidence
of shared revenue / shared inventory / shared accounting does it climb.

**Plead in the alternative; don't lead with it.**

### 1.9 Zabezpieczenie roszczenia (art. 730 + art. 747 KPC) is the highest-leverage early move

Unanimous. The risk that Waldek transfers his JDG assets on receiving
service of the pozew is real — he has a 17-year established business with
liquid accounts on Biała Lista, inventory, and potentially Kraków real
estate. The civil interim measure has two requirements:

1. *Uprawdopodobnienie roszczenia* — prima facie showing of the claim.
   The email thread (Waldek's written refund promise on
   waldek@gpucomputer.pl) plus the Wayback co-marketing snapshots from
   2016–2019 plus the live 2026 gpucomputer.pl/kontakt page are
   sufficient.
2. *Interes prawny* — risk that enforcement would be impossible or
   significantly hindered without security. Mateusz's pre-existing
   Eindhoven flight is the leading evidence; Waldek's potential mirror move
   is the realistic concern.

Available measures (art. 747 KPC): freezing JDG bank accounts on Biała
Lista, compulsory mortgage (*hipoteka przymusowa*) on any identified
Kraków real estate, seizure of inventory and receivables. The motion is
decided **ex parte within 7 days** (statutory; often 2–3 weeks in
practice) and executed by the komornik **before** service of the pozew —
this is the critical sequencing that defeats asset-flight risk.

Filing fee: 100 PLN. The single cheapest piece of insurance available
on this case.

### 1.10 Supplementary criminal complaint naming Waldek as co-perpetrator

Unanimous strategic value, with slightly different emphases:

- File supplementary *zawiadomienie* under art. 304 § 1 KPK naming Waldek
  as co-perpetrator (*współsprawca*, art. 18 § 1 KK) or aider (*pomocnik*)
  on the underlying art. 286 § 1 KK fraud.
- Threshold for the prosecutor to interrogate Waldek as suspect rather
  than witness is *uzasadnione podejrzenie* (reasonable suspicion) under
  art. 71–74 KPK — a relatively low bar that the email evidence likely
  meets.
- Once Waldek is *podejrzany*, the prosecutor can issue *zabezpieczenie
  majątkowe* under art. 291–295 KPK — a criminal asset freeze that is
  faster, broader, and costs the creditor nothing (state-funded). This is
  the same asset-freeze tool that the criminal-law synthesis identifies
  for Mateusz; expanding the complaint to Waldek puts both inside the
  freeze envelope.
- The criminal track does not compete with the civil track. They are
  parallel and complementary; the criminal track adds investigative
  reach (bank-record subpoena, JPK_VAT access) that civil discovery in
  Poland cannot replicate. See `../../crime-law/synthesis-criminal-law-angle.md`
  for the underlying criminal mapping.

**One procedural caveat (Perplexity Computer):** KPK art. 415 § 1 zdanie
drugie prohibits the criminal court from ordering art. 46 KK restitution
where the same claim is the subject of a parallel civil proceeding. If
art. 46 restitution is wanted, the civil case may need to be limited or
withdrawn at the appropriate moment — a tactical call for the lawyer.

### 1.11 Actio pauliana (art. 527–534 KC) is held in reserve

Unanimous. The theory is *legally strong on the law*, especially because:

- Art. 527 § 3 KC creates a **rebuttable presumption** that a person in a
  close relationship with the debtor knew of the harmful intent. Łopata
  family relationship satisfies *bliski stosunek*.
- Art. 527 § 4 KC creates a **parallel presumption** for entrepreneurs in
  permanent business relations with the debtor. The decade of
  gpucomputer.pl co-marketing satisfies *stały stosunek gospodarczy*.
- Together, these presumptions effectively reverse the burden of proof on
  the bad-faith element.

But it is *factually empty today* — no specific asset transfer has been
identified between the two JDGs. Without a concrete *czynność prawna*
(bank transfer, inventory shift, invoice flow, real-estate move), there
is no pauliana claim to file. The right timing is **after** komornik
documents *bezskuteczność egzekucji* against Mateusz and/or the
criminal-track investigation surfaces transfer evidence in bank or
JPK_VAT records.

Note (Perplexity Pro): art. 13f UKSC provides a special fixed fee of
1,000 PLN for pauliańska suits brought after the underlying claim has a
qualifying final title. This further argues for the post-judgment timing.

### 1.12 Mateusz-only recovery is materially constrained

All four assess Mateusz's Polish enforcement surface as thin (virtual
office, no employees, no fleet, no Polish real estate surfaced) and his
recoverable income as a Dutch factory wage attached via EAPO + Dutch
*gerechtsdeurwaarder*. Net Dutch wage approximately €2,000–2,500/month;
Dutch protected minimum subsistence shields a large portion; realistic
garnishment 10–20 % monthly; full recovery of 155,000 PLN (~€36,000) from
wages alone would take several years.

**One factual correction (ChatGPT 5.5 Pro):** EAPO is a *bank-account*
preservation order under Regulation 655/2014, not a wage-garnishment
tool. Dutch wage garnishment requires a separate Dutch enforcement step
after recognition of the Polish title under Brussels I bis (Reg.
1215/2012) or as a European Enforcement Order (Reg. 805/2004 — only
available for uncontested claims). EAPO can freeze whatever balance
accumulates in Mateusz's Dutch bank account between paydays, which is
useful but limited.

## 2. Where the sources diverge

### 2.1 The probability that art. 422 KC succeeds against Waldek

| Source | Probability range | Limiting factor cited |
|---|---|---|
| ChatGPT 5.5 Pro | 20–35 % weak emails; **45–65 % favorable emails**; 65 %+ with state evidence | Wants pre-filing email review as gate |
| Perplexity Pro | **55–70 %** | Treats SN II CSKP 607/23 as decisive lowering of the bar |
| Perplexity Computer | **20–35 %** | Treats the *knowledge* element as the bottleneck (art. 422 in fine requires actual knowledge of benefiting from another's tort, not constructive) |
| Gemini | **High** (no numeric; treats as strongest available theory) | None binding |

**Best read:** Perplexity Computer is correct that the *knowing benefit*
limb (świadome skorzystanie ze szkody) requires actual subjective
knowledge per SN III CZP 89/17. But Perplexity Pro and Gemini are also
right that the **aiding (pomocnictwo)** limb is the better fit on the
facts, and SN II CSKP 607/23 (2025) confirms that art. 422 aiding
liability extends to *dolus eventualis* — Waldek's awareness that his
representations *could* facilitate the harm and his acceptance of that
risk is sufficient. The email thread is the gate. If Waldek's written
refund promise was made after he knew or should have known delivery
wasn't going to happen, the aiding limb is met. **Realistic range with
the documented email evidence: 40–60 %.** This is materially better than
betting on Mateusz alone.

### 2.2 Parallel suits vs. unified post-discontinuance refile

| Source | Recommendation | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| ChatGPT 5.5 Pro | Keep EPU + separate Waldek suit (parallel) | Preserves EPU optionality; cleanest |
| Perplexity Pro | Keep EPU + simultaneous Waldek suit + zabezpieczenie (parallel) | Speed; doesn't gamble on EPU outcome |
| Perplexity Computer | Parallel, but expects EPU to collapse on service to Mateusz | Pragmatic |
| Gemini | **Let EPU collapse → file one unified suit in Kraków within 3-month window** | Fee credit + single trial + single judgment |

**Best read:** This is the most important divergence and the most
defensible call for the lawyer. Gemini's recommendation is procedurally
elegant but front-loads two risks: (a) waiting for the EPU discontinuance
order gives Waldek months of warning to move assets — exactly what the
zabezpieczenie is designed to prevent; (b) the 3-month bridge runs from
the discontinuance order, which itself is at the e-court's discretion
and could come in 4–9 months depending on how the service-abroad issue is
handled.

The **parallel approach** (ChatGPT, Perplexity Pro, Perplexity Computer)
is safer for asset preservation: filing zabezpieczenie + Waldek pozew
*now* freezes Waldek's assets before he knows the suit is coming. The
2,000–3,000 PLN fee differential vs. the unified path is cheap insurance
against asset flight.

**Recommended sequencing:**

1. Now — file zabezpieczenie (ex parte) + separate Waldek pozew in Sąd
   Okręgowy Kraków, on art. 422 KC theory with spółka cywilna in the
   alternative.
2. Continue monitoring EPU. If the e-court issues the discontinuance
   order, refile the Mateusz claim within 3 months in Sąd Okręgowy
   Kraków under art. 505³⁷ § 2 (preserves 2026-04-08 priority).
3. Once both cases are pending in SO Kraków, motion under art. 219 KPC to
   connect them for joint examination. **Caveat (Gemini, citing SN
   IV CZ 24/10):** art. 219 connection is purely technical — it does not
   merge claims, and each case retains independent appeals. The
   "unified judgment" benefit is administrative, not procedural.

### 2.5 Whether to wait for Mateusz's sprzeciw

ChatGPT and Perplexity Pro consider this — pre-2020 the sprzeciw-then-amend

sprzeciw triggers discontinuance under art. 505³⁶ KPC. So the "wait for
sprzeciw" tactic is no longer a meaningful option. All four converge on
not waiting.
