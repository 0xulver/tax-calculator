### B.2 Subjective Amendment (Adding Waldemar) in EPU — Is It Possible?

**Short answer: No, not in EPU. The mechanism does not exist there.**

KPC art. 505²⁸ states that EPU proceedings apply the *postępowanie upominawcze* rules with specific exceptions. The provisions governing subjective change of parties (KPC art. 194–198: *przypozwanie*, *wstąpienie*, and *zmiana podmiotowa*) apply to the ordinary *postępowanie procesowe*. EPU is an exclusively electronic, simplified procedure. KPC art. 505³¹ § 1 explicitly states that **procedural documents from the claimant not submitted electronically have no legal effect**. The EPU system has no electronic input pathway for subjective amendments involving new parties.

Furthermore, KPC art. 505²⁸ § 2 limits EPU to defendants serv­able in Poland. Waldemar Łopata is Kraków-resident and would satisfy this requirement — but the structural barrier is that the EPU system has no mechanism for multi-defendant cases where defendants have different legal personalities (two separate JDGs). The procedural literature (Ereciński, *Kodeks postępowania cywilnego. Komentarz*, t. III, Wolters Kluwer 2022) treats EPU as inherently single-debtor compatible in practice, with no judicial procedure for mid-case defendant addition.

**Conclusion: You cannot add Waldemar Łopata to the existing EPU case.**

## Section A — Statutory Map of Joint Liability Theories

### H1 — Implied/De Facto Spółka Cywilna (KC art. 860–875, solidary liability under art. 864)

**Statutory text:**

> **KC art. 860 § 1:** *"Przez umowę spółki wspólnicy zobowiązują się dążyć do osiągnięcia wspólnego celu gospodarczego przez działanie w sposób oznaczony, w szczególności przez wniesienie wkładów."*  
> ("By a civil partnership agreement the partners undertake to pursue a common economic goal by specified action, in particular by making contributions.")

> **KC art. 860 § 2:** *"Umowa spółki powinna być stwierdzona pismem."*  
> ("The partnership agreement should be evidenced in writing.")

> **KC art. 864:** *"Za zobowiązania spółki cywilnej wspólnicy odpowiedzialni są solidarnie."*  
> ("Partners are jointly and severally liable for the obligations of the civil partnership.")

**Elements plaintiff must prove:**

1. An agreement — express or implied — to pursue a **common economic goal**;
2. Specified **joint action** by both parties (not merely parallel activity);
3. **Contributions** by each party (money, services, assets, know-how);
4. The obligation in question arose **while both were partners**.

**Strength of fit: MODERATE (trending toward Weak without additional evidence)**

*For:* The factual pattern is striking. From 2016 to the present, gpucomputer.pl publicly presents "3dkrakow" (Waldemar's JDG, NIP 7752083995) and "GPUCOMPUTER" (Mateusz's JDG) as two complementary arms of one operation — Wayback snapshots from 2016, 2018, 2019 show joint *o-nas* ("about us") content, and the 2026 live site still lists Waldemar's email `waldek@gpucomputer.pl` and phone in the "Konfiguracje" (Configuration) section. Waldemar personally negotiated specs, deadlines, and made the explicit written refund promise in your email thread — this is the kind of conduct that in substance amounts to a contribution of services (art. 860 § 1: *"działanie w sposób oznaczony"*). The surname linkage reinforces the inference of a pre-existing business relationship likely organized in advance.

*Against:* Polish law treats spółka cywilna as a consensual contract that "should be evidenced in writing" (art. 860 § 2). While the *powinno* formulation means absence of writing does not automatically invalidate the agreement (it is an imperfect obligation, not a validity requirement — see Osajda, *KC Komentarz*, 2024, art. 860 Nb. 9), courts are deeply reluctant to infer a spółka cywilna from external commercial conduct between two separately registered entrepreneurs. There is no CEIDG entry for a spółka cywilna between them, no joint NIP, no wspólny rachunek bankowy in evidence, no Wayback snapshot showing the words "spółka cywilna." The SA w Szczecinie (I AGa 203/18, 10 December 2018) distinguished cooperative business relations from a spółka cywilna, holding that even tight operational integration does not create one without the structural elements of shared profit and joint decision-making.

**Standard of proof:** Civil standard — *uprawdopodobnienie* is sufficient for interim measures; full evidentiary *udowodnienie* for trial on the merits.

**Evidence needed:**

- (Already provable) Email archive: Waldemar negotiating, promising delivery, promising refund — evidences contribution of services and participation in joint enterprise;
- (Already provable) Wayback snapshots 2016/2018/2019 of gpucomputer.pl showing joint "o nas" content;
- (Already provable) 2026 live gpucomputer.pl showing Waldemar's contact data — evidences continuing joint operation;
- (Needs discovery/state investigation) Shared bank account or shared payment processor; shared software licenses or shared premises lease; how VAT/JPK_V7 was filed — whether invoices from 3dkrakow went through the GPUcomputer NIP or vice versa; **only tax authorities (KAS) can access JPK files**;
- (Needs discovery) Whether they jointly bid on suppliers (B2B purchase orders listing both names);
- (Needs state investigation) Whether ZUS treated Waldemar as an employee vs independent contractor — if he was paid a salary by Mateusz, that weakens the spółka cywilna argument and strengthens H4.

**Leading SN authority:** SN II CSK 189/09 (16 September 2009) confirmed that a spółka cywilna does not require formal written agreement to be legally effective — the written form is *ad probationem*, not *ad solemnitatem* — but stressed that proof of the common-goal element and the joint-action element must still be established factually. SA w Białymstoku I AGa 81/19 (21 November 2019) confirmed art. 864 applies to all partners who were members at the time the obligation arose.

**Honest probability of success before SO Kraków:** 25–40%. The co-marketing evidence is compelling circumstantially, but a Kraków judge will demand more than a shared website and shared surname to hold two separately-registered entrepreneurs jointly and severally liable on an art. 864 basis. This theory is viable as a pleaded alternative claim but should not be the primary basis.

### H2 — Joint Tortfeasor Liability (KC art. 422 + art. 441)

**Statutory text:**

> **KC art. 422:** *"Za szkodę odpowiedzialny jest nie tylko ten, kto ją bezpośrednio wyrządził, lecz także ten, kto inną osobę do wyrządzenia szkody nakłonił albo był jej pomocny, jak również ten, kto świadomie skorzystał z wyrządzonej drugiemu szkody."*  
> ("Liability for damage rests not only on the person who caused it directly but also on the person who induced another to cause the damage or assisted in causing it, as well as the person who knowingly benefited from the damage caused to another.")

> **KC art. 441 § 1:** *"Jeżeli kilka osób ponosi odpowiedzialność za szkodę wyrządzoną czynem niedozwolonym, ich odpowiedzialność jest solidarna."*  
> ("If several persons are liable for damage caused by a tort, their liability is joint and several.")

> **KC art. 415:** *"Kto z winy swej wyrządził drugiemu szkodę, obowiązany jest do jej naprawienia."*  
> ("Anyone who by their own fault causes damage to another is obliged to repair it.")

**Elements plaintiff must prove:**

1. A tort (delikt) — not merely contractual non-performance;
2. Waldemar either: (a) **induced** Mateusz to commit the tort, or (b) **aided** in committing it, or (c) **knowingly benefited** from the damage to Magnus;
3. Causation between Waldemar's participation and Magnus's loss (155,000 PLN);
4. **For "knowing benefit" (art. 422 in fine):** Actual subjective knowledge that the benefit derived from someone else's wrongful act — negligence is insufficient; the SN (III CZP 89/17, 21 December 2017, OSNC 2018/10/96) and earlier SN V CSK 599/14 (3 June 2015) confirmed this is a strict knowledge requirement, not constructive or imputed knowledge.

**Strength of fit: MODERATE (knowledge element is the bottleneck)**

*For:* The foundational tort here is likely **fraudulent misrepresentation** (oszustwo cywilne / dolus in contrahendo, KC art. 415 + art. 84 § 1) rather than mere non-performance. Under SN V KK 240/17 (6 December 2017), if a vendor knowingly conceals an objective situation affecting the ability to perform — including pre-existing financial inability — this satisfies the *wprowadzenie w błąd* (deception) element. Applied civilly under art. 415: if Mateusz knew at the time of contracting (January 2026) that he could not deliver the workstation, and Waldemar knew this too (by virtue of operating within the same enterprise), Waldemar's participation in negotiating specs and promising refunds could constitute "aiding" (*pomocnictwo*). The critical piece is your email archive: **Waldemar's written refund promise, made after the delivery failure, is evidence that he was involved in both the original deceptive transaction and the subsequent attempt to manage (and delay) the fallout.**

*Against:* Art. 422 does not permit use of the close-person presumption from art. 527 § 3 (skarga pauliańska). As [Strategie Procesowe commentary](https://strategieprocesowe.pl/2020/05/21/odpowiedzialnosc-cywilna-osoby-trzeciej-ktora-skorzystala-ze-szkody-art-422-k-c/) explicitly notes, the *knowing benefit* prong requires **intentional fault (wina umyślna)** — you must prove actual knowledge, not that Waldemar "should have known." The "inducer" prong (*nakłonił*) requires showing Waldemar actively pushed Mateusz toward the wrongful act. Unless emails show Waldemar organizing the sales process knowing the enterprise was unable to deliver, this is hard to establish.

**Standard of proof:** Full civil evidentiary standard (*udowodnienie*) for knowledge; circumstantial inference (art. 231 KPC) is permissible if direct evidence is unavailable.

**Evidence needed:**

- (Already provable) Email thread: Waldemar negotiated specs pre-contract — positions him as a participant in the transaction;
- (Already provable) Waldemar's written refund promise — shows awareness that a harm occurred and that he has a role in remedying it;
- (Conditionally provable) Whether Waldemar received any portion of the 155,000 PLN — if yes, the "knowing benefit" limb is much stronger;
- (Needs state investigation) Bank records showing money flow from Mateusz's account to Waldemar's 3dkrakow account — only prosecutors or KAS can compel this.

**Leading SN authority:** SN III CSK 190/16 (18 May 2017); SN I CSK 507/09 (16 June 2010); SA w Warszawie V ACa 1441/17 (7 December 2018, LEX nr 2613475).

**Honest probability before SO Kraków:** 20–35%. This theory is viable if the email archive is strong and shows Waldemar receiving consideration for his involvement. Without proof of actual knowledge or a money flow to Waldemar, a Kraków judge is likely to find the evidence insufficient on the mental-state element.

### H3 — Civil Consequence of Criminal Co-Perpetration (KK art. 18 § 1 + art. 286 → art. 46 KK)

**Statutory text:**

> **KK art. 18 § 1:** *"Odpowiada za sprawstwo nie tylko ten, kto wykonuje czyn zabroniony sam albo wspólnie i w porozumieniu z inną osobą, ale także ten, kto kieruje wykonaniem czynu zabronionego przez inną osobę lub wykorzystując uzależnienie innej osoby od siebie, poleca jej wykonanie takiego czynu."*  
> ("Criminal perpetration is committed not only by the person who carries out the prohibited act alone or jointly and in concert with another, but also by the person who directs another person in executing the prohibited act or, exploiting another's dependence, orders them to carry it out.")

> **KK art. 286 § 1:** *"Kto, w celu osiągnięcia korzyści majątkowej, doprowadza inną osobę do niekorzystnego rozporządzenia własnym albo cudzym mieniem za pomocą wprowadzenia jej w błąd albo wyzyskania błędu lub niezdolności do należytego pojmowania przedsiębranego działania, podlega karze pozbawienia wolności od 6 miesięcy do lat 8."*  
> ("Anyone who, for the purpose of obtaining financial benefit, induces another person to make an unfavorable disposition of their own or another's property by deceiving them, exploiting their error, or exploiting their inability to comprehend the action undertaken, is liable to imprisonment from 6 months to 8 years.")

> **KK art. 46 § 1:** *"W razie skazania sąd może orzec, a na wniosek pokrzywdzonego lub innej osoby uprawnionej orzeka, stosując przepisy prawa cywilnego, obowiązek naprawienia, w całości albo w części, wyrządzonej przestępstwem szkody."*  
> ("Upon conviction, the court may, and upon application of the aggrieved party or other authorised person must, applying civil-law rules, order the offender to repair, in whole or in part, the damage caused by the offence.")

**Elements for criminal fraud in prepaid order context (from SN V KK 240/17):**

1. Deception — concealment of an objective circumstance affecting ability to perform (e.g., financial inability at the time of contracting);
2. Induced unfavorable property disposition — Magnus's 155,000 PLN prepayment;
3. Purpose of financial gain;
4. For co-perpetration under art. 18 § 1: joint plan (*porozumienie*) and division of roles.

**Procedural track:**

If Magnus has already filed a criminal complaint (*zawiadomienie o przestępstwie*) against Mateusz, **adding Waldemar as a suspect requires a separate act by the investigative authority** — the prokuratura cannot be forced by the complainant to extend suspicion. The correct procedural step is:

1. **Submit a supplemental written statement** to the prokuratura handling the investigation (or the policja conducting it on their behalf), expressly naming Waldemar Łopata, NIP 7752083995, ul. Św. Filipa 23/4 Kraków, with the factual basis (his role in negotiations, refund promise, shared website operation, surname linkage);
2. The prokuratura will decide whether to extend the investigation and issue a *postanowienie o przedstawieniu zarzutów* (indictment decision) to Waldemar;
3. **Waldemar remains a świadek (witness) until formally charged** — he cannot be criminally asset-preserved (KPK art. 291) until he acquires suspect status;
4. Once Waldemar is a suspect, the prosecutor may issue a *postanowienie o zabezpieczeniu majątkowym* under KPK art. 291, which takes effect immediately as an enforcement title and can attach his Kraków JDG bank accounts, inventory, and real estate.

**Criminal art. 46 restitution vs civil claim:** Under KPK art. 415 § 1 zdanie drugie, the criminal court **cannot** order art. 46 restitution if the civil claim is already the subject of another proceeding (*ne bis in idem* for the same claim). Your EPU filing creates this bar. However, you may **withdraw the civil proceedings** or limit the civil claim to allow the criminal court to order full restitution — a tactical decision for your Polish attorney.

**Strength of fit: MODERATE for criminal track; WEAK for using criminal conviction to accelerate civil judgment**

**Honest probability:** The art. 286 charge is plausible given: (i) asset-light JDG that kept taking custom orders; (ii) Waldemar's post-failure refund promise (suggesting knowledge of inability to deliver); (iii) continued operation of the website. But criminal proceedings in Poland are slow (18–48 months to indictment; 24–60 months to conviction), making this track irrelevant to near-term recovery against Waldemar. Use the criminal complaint as a **pressure instrument** and for asset preservation, not as the primary recovery mechanism.

### H4 — Apparent Authority / Agency by Estoppel (KC art. 97 / 103 / 105) and Joint Contractual Liability

**Statutory text:**

> **KC art. 97:** *"Osobę czynną w lokalu przedsiębiorstwa przeznaczonym do obsługiwania publiczności poczytuje się w razie wątpliwości za umocowaną do dokonywania czynności prawnych, które zazwyczaj bywają dokonywane z osobami korzystającymi z usług tego przedsiębiorstwa."*  
> ("A person acting in a business premises open to the public is presumed, in case of doubt, to be authorised to perform legal acts customarily performed with persons using that business's services.")

> **KC art. 103 § 1:** *"Jeżeli zawierający umowę jako pełnomocnik nie ma umocowania albo przekroczy jego zakres, ważność umowy zależy od jej potwierdzenia przez osobę, w której imieniu umowa została zawarta."*  
> ("If a person concluding a contract as agent lacks authority or exceeds it, the contract's validity depends on ratification by the person in whose name the contract was concluded.")

**Analysis of fit:**

Art. 97 KC creates ostensible (apparent) authority for persons acting within a business premises of a business open to the public. The conceptual problem in this case is that Waldemar did not act within Mateusz's business premises — he negotiated via email from his own JDG position. Art. 97 targets shop-floor employees, not co-owners of sister enterprises.

**The stronger sub-theory here is joint contractual liability through "joint principalship" (*wspólne zamawianie przez dwóch przedsiębiorców*):** If the court finds that Waldemar, by publicly co-presenting himself as part of the GPUcomputer enterprise, and by actively negotiating the contract terms (specs, delivery date, refund), became a *co-party to the contract* in substance — not as agent but as a co-contracting principal — then both are contractual debtors. This is not a recognized doctrine with a statutory label in Polish law, but it is analytically available through:

1. Teleological interpretation of the contracting sequence: Magnus contracted with "GPUcomputer," which presented itself as an operation encompassing both Mateusz and Waldemar;
2. Art. 65 § 2 KC (interpretation of contracts by their purpose and parties' intentions);
3. Art. 56 KC (legal act produces effects arising from the law, principles of social coexistence, and established custom).

**Strength of fit: WEAK as a standalone theory, MODERATE as a reinforcing argument**

The doctrine would require evidence that at the time of contracting, a reasonable person would understand they were contracting with both parties jointly. The email trail — if Waldemar conducted all specifications negotiations while Mateusz issued the invoice — creates a plausible case. However, Polish courts (unlike common-law courts) have no "joint venture by estoppel" doctrine and will demand a clear legal basis for holding Waldemar personally liable on the contract.

**Evidence needed:**

- (Already provable) Email showing Waldemar negotiating specs before invoice was issued;
- (Already provable) Website presenting both as one enterprise;
- (Conditionally provable) Whether the invoice or order confirmation referenced "3dkrakow" or Waldemar's NIP anywhere.

### H5 — Skarga Pauliańska / Actio Pauliana (KC art. 527 ff.)

**Statutory text:**

> **KC art. 527 § 1:** *"Gdy wskutek czynności prawnej dłużnika dokonanej z pokrzywdzeniem wierzycieli osoba trzecia uzyskała korzyść majątkową, każdy z wierzycieli może żądać uznania tej czynności za bezskuteczną w stosunku do niego, jeżeli dłużnik działał ze świadomością pokrzywdzenia wierzycieli, a osoba trzecia o tym wiedziała lub przy zachowaniu należytej staranności mogła się dowiedzieć."*  
> ("Where a legal act of the debtor prejudicial to creditors has caused a third party to obtain a financial benefit, any creditor may demand that the act be declared ineffective against them, if the debtor acted with awareness of prejudicing creditors, and the third party knew or could have known this with due diligence.")

> **KC art. 527 § 3:** *"Jeżeli wskutek czynności prawnej dłużnika dokonanej z pokrzywdzeniem wierzycieli uzyskała korzyść majątkową osoba będąca w bliskim z nim stosunku, domniemywa się, że osoba ta wiedziała, iż dłużnik działał ze świadomością pokrzywdzenia wierzycieli."*  
> ("If a person in a close relationship with the debtor obtained a financial benefit from the debtor's act prejudicing creditors, it is presumed that that person knew the debtor acted with awareness of prejudicing creditors.")

> **KC art. 534:** Limitation: skarga pauliańska must be filed within **5 years** of the legal act being challenged.

**Elements plaintiff must prove:**

1. You have a valid claim against Mateusz (✓ — pending EPU);
2. Mateusz made a **legal act** (e.g., transferred assets, paid money, granted a mortgage) that prejudiced you;
3. That act benefited Waldemar materially;
4. Mateusz was aware of prejudicing creditors;
5. Waldemar either knew of this (or, if the close-person presumption under art. 527 § 3 applies, is presumed to have known).

**Close-person presumption:** If the family relationship (Waldemar = father or uncle of Mateusz, inferred from the *Łopata* surname in Mateusz's hyphenated name *Szklarski-Łopata*) is confirmed, art. 527 § 3 creates a **rebuttable presumption** that Waldemar knew of the prejudice to creditors. This substantially reduces your evidentiary burden. SN I CSK 323/12 (15 February 2013) confirmed the *wierzytelność* need not be liquidated at the time of filing; it suffices that it is not hypothetical.

**Critical pre-condition:** Skarga pauliańska requires that there be a specific **legal act to challenge**. You must identify a concrete transfer: e.g., Mateusz transferred GPUcomputer inventory, intellectual property, customer lists, or funds to Waldemar's 3dkrakow enterprise, and this transfer occurred after January 2026 (or is continuing). At present, without evidence of a specific transfer, this theory is not yet actionable. It becomes most powerful **after komornik bezskuteczność** — i.e., after the komornik confirms that Mateusz has no seizable assets in Poland, at which point you can challenge any prior transfer to Waldemar.

**Timing recommendation:** Do NOT file actio pauliana now. File it **after** obtaining the nakaz zapłaty against Mateusz and after the komornik's asset search (zlecenie poszukiwania majątku, art. 801² KPC, fee: 100 PLN) reveals bezskuteczność or near-bezskuteczność. That sequence will also give you the documented insolvency (*niewypłacalność*) required under art. 527 § 2.

**Strength of fit: STRONG (as a second-stage strategy) / Not Yet Actionable**

**Honest probability, if properly sequenced:** 65–80% if a concrete transfer to Waldemar can be identified and the family relationship is proven.

### H6 — Other Theories

#### H6a — KC art. 415 + art. 5 (Abuse of Right / Culpa in Contrahendo)

**KC art. 5:** *"Nie można czynić ze swego prawa użytku, który by był sprzeczny ze społeczno-gospodarczym przeznaczeniem tego prawa lub z zasadami współżycia społecznego. Takie działanie lub zaniechanie uprawnionego nie jest uważane za wykonywanie prawa i nie korzysta z ochrony."*

Art. 5 is a defensive mechanism — it prevents one party from exercising a legal right in a socially abusive manner. It does not **create** a liability basis. Polish courts (SN II CSK 605/10; SA Kraków I ACa 1265/13) are consistent that art. 5 cannot substitute for a primary legal basis of liability. It can supplement an art. 415 or art. 422 claim as a reinforcing argument.

**Strength of fit: NOT APPLICABLE as standalone basis; supporting argument only.**

#### H6b — KC art. 84/88 — Deceit and Voidability of the Contract

**KC art. 84 § 1:** Allows a party whose declaration of will was made under a material *błąd* (error) to avoid the legal effect of that declaration (rescission), not damages. Art. 86 § 1 (podstęp — deceit): where the error was induced by the other party's deceit, rescission is available even if the error was not material. Art. 88 § 1: rescission must be exercised within **1 year** of discovering the deceit.

This track gives Magnus the right to **rescind the contract** and recover 155,000 PLN as *nienależne świadczenie* (unjust enrichment, art. 405 ff. KC). The problem is that rescission can only be declared against the contract party — Mateusz/GPUcomputer, not Waldemar — unless the court finds Waldemar was also a contracting party. Art. 84/88 reinforces the restitution claim against Mateusz but does not create Waldemar's liability directly.

**Strength of fit: WEAK as basis for Waldemar's liability; relevant for strengthening rescission claim against Mateusz.**

#### H6c — Przebicie zasłony / Enterprise-veil piercing between two JDGs

Polish law has no general statutory doctrine of "piercing the veil" for natural persons operating as JDGs — this doctrine exists in company law (sp. z o.o., SA) and even there is narrowly construed (SN I CSK 151/14, 8 October 2015). A JDG is a natural person; you are suing the natural person directly. The "veil" that exists in corporate law does not apply. There is no theoretical room for veil-piercing between two JDGs — you are already suing the person *behind* the enterprise (Waldemar Łopata as an individual who operates "WALDEMAR ŁOPATA 3d Kraków"). The correct path is one of the theories above, not piercing a non-existent veil.

**Strength of fit: NOT APPLICABLE.**

#### H6d — UOKiK / Consumer Law Joint Liability

The UOKiK (Office of Competition and Consumer Protection) can act against traders who use unfair commercial practices (ustawa z dn. 23 sierpnia 2007 r. o przeciwdziałaniu nieuczciwym praktykom rynkowym). However, UOKiK proceedings result in administrative fines and injunctions, not civil damages owed to individual consumers. They are a **pressure/reputation tool**, not a recovery mechanism. Magnus may file a complaint with UOKiK naming both enterprises as jointly operating a misleading commercial practice, but should not expect financial recovery from this avenue.

**Strength of fit: WEAK as recovery mechanism; useful as reputational/investigative tool.**
