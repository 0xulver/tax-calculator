# Asset Flow Between the Two JDGs + Actio Pauliana — Research Prompt

## Purpose

Targeted research prompt covering the second strategic angle on Waldemar: not
"is he a joint tortfeasor on the original claim" (that's
`00-joint-liability-research-brief.md`), but **"can I reach his assets via the
fraudulent-transfer route (actio pauliana, KC art. 527 ff.) if money or
inventory was moved from Mateusz's GPUcomputer JDG to Waldemar's 3d Kraków
JDG"**.

These are different theories. Joint tortfeasor liability puts Waldemar on the
judgment from the start. Actio pauliana works *backwards*: once a judgment
exists against Mateusz, I can attack transfers Mateusz made to Waldemar
that defeat enforcement. Both can be pursued, in different sequences.

Target agents: ChatGPT 5.5 Pro, Perplexity Pro, Gemini 3.1 Deep Research.

## What I want covered

### 1. The statutory route — KC art. 527–534

For each of the operative articles, give the Polish text + English
translation, the elements, and how each maps to my facts:

- **Art. 527 § 1 KC** — bad-faith transfer (czynność prawna z
  pokrzywdzeniem wierzycieli) where third party knew or should have known
- **Art. 527 § 3 KC** — *presumption of bad faith* if transferee is a
  close person (*osoba pozostająca w bliskim stosunku*) of the debtor.
  Father / uncle / brother / cousin all qualify. **This is the linchpin
  if the Łopata family hypothesis is confirmed.**
- **Art. 527 § 4 KC** — presumption if transferee is a business person
  in a permanent commercial relationship with the debtor. Independent of
  the family link, the 17-year-old shared-domain co-marketing of
  gpucomputer.pl by both JDGs satisfies *stały stosunek gospodarczy*.
  Confirm.
- **Art. 528 KC** — gratuitous transfer (*czynność prawna bezpłatna*):
  no knowledge required of transferee at all. If any transfer was below
  market, presume gratuitous element.
- **Art. 530 KC** — future creditors: transfer made with intent to harm
  creditors who didn't yet exist at the time of transfer. Less useful for
  me but relevant if Mateusz pre-emptively moved value before taking my
  155k.
- **Art. 531 § 1 KC** — judgment / nakaz: the court declares the transfer
  ineffective *toward the plaintiff*, not voided generally. The creditor
  then satisfies the claim out of the transferred asset by ordinary
  enforcement.
- **Art. 534 KC** — limitation: **5 years from the date of the transfer**.
  Hard limit.

### 5. Sequencing — when to file actio pauliana

Under art. 532 KC, the action is filed by the **creditor** against the
**transferee** (not the debtor). Sequencing options:

- **Before judgment against Mateusz**: art. 532 KC arguably allows this
  on the *roszczenie* alone, before *tytuł egzekucyjny*. Confirm with
  current case law.
- **Concurrently with the underlying suit**: pozew under art. 527 KC as
  a separate proceeding running parallel to the EPU / Kraków suit
  against Mateusz.
- **After judgment + after enforcement bezskuteczność**: the classical
  sequence — komornik first documents that ordinary enforcement against
  Mateusz fails, then I file actio pauliana against Waldemar. Slowest
  but most evidence-supported route.

### 7. Interaction with the criminal track

If the criminal track adds Waldemar as a co-suspect under art. 286 KK

liability for receiving the transferred asset, separate from civil actio
pauliana but on the same fact basis.

Specifically:
- **Art. 300 § 1 KK** — debtor (Mateusz) frustrates creditor satisfaction

  knowingly received the asset to shield it.
- **Art. 300 § 2 KK** — aggravated form once an enforcement title exists.
  Triggers after EPU nakaz issues.
- **Art. 300 § 3 KK** — multiple-creditor aggravation if other GPUcomputer
  victims surface.

Does the criminal-track route to attach Waldemar's assets via art. 291 KPK
*zabezpieczenie majątkowe* (asset freeze in criminal procedure) supersede
or supplement the civil actio pauliana? Which moves faster?
