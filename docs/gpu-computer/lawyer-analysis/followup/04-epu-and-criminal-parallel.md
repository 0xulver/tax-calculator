# Prompt 4 — Running the EPU civil claim and the criminal case in parallel

## Role
You are a Polish litigator who handles parallel civil and criminal tracks. Explain whether I can run both at once, how they interact, and whether I can end up "winning" both — and crucially whether I can collect the money more than once.

## Case facts
- Civil track: EPU payment-order claim already filed 08.04.2026, **Nc-e 552126/26**, Sąd Rejonowy Lublin-Zachód, for 155,000 PLN + statutory delay interest from 31.03.2026 against Mateusz Szklarski / JDG GPUcomputer. No nakaz zapłaty issued yet as of late May 2026.
- Criminal track: I am considering a zawiadomienie under art. 286 § 1 KK against Mateusz (and possibly Waldek — see prompt 3).
- Debtor lives in the Netherlands; the lawyer favours letting EPU run because of its fiction-of-service advantage (fikcja doręczenia) and advises against withdrawing it to seek a civil asset freeze.

## What I need

1. **Can both proceed simultaneously?** Confirm there is no procedural bar to having an open EPU/civil claim and a criminal complaint for the same facts at the same time.

2. **Will the civil/EPU case be suspended because of the criminal case?**
   - **art. 177 § 1 pkt 4 KPC** (zawieszenie postępowania cywilnego pending criminal) — is suspension discretionary or mandatory, and how likely is it for a documentary EPU claim? Does it matter who requests it (me vs. the debtor as a stalling tactic)?
   - If the debtor files a sprzeciw and the EPU converts to ordinary proceedings, does the suspension risk rise?
   - Can the criminal case instead be *helped* by a civil judgment (or vice versa)?

3. **Can I hold both a civil execution title AND a criminal restitution order for the same 155k?**
   - How do a civil nakaz zapłaty / judgment and an **art. 46 KK** obowiązek naprawienia szkody coexist?
   - The anti-double-recovery principle: if I obtain both, can I enforce both, or only collect the 155k once? What happens to the second title once the debt is satisfied? (powaga rzeczy osądzonej / zakaz podwójnego zaspokojenia)
   - Is there any *advantage* to holding two titles (e.g. one enforceable in Poland, one with the non-dischargeability effect of prompt 7)?

4. **Timing/sequencing strategy.** Given the debtor is abroad and KRZ is clean, what is the optimal ordering — e.g. push EPU to a final nakaz first (for the execution title and the bezskuteczność evidence), file the criminal complaint in parallel for the asset-freeze and pressure, then use whichever bites? Does filing criminal *now* vs. *after* the nakaz change anything?

5. **The wniosek o przyspieszenie rozpoznania sprawy** the lawyer suggested for EPU — is it worth filing, what does it say, and does it realistically speed up Lublin-Zachód?

## Output format
Direct yes/no answers up front, then the suspension analysis, then the double-recovery rule, then a recommended sequencing of the two tracks.
