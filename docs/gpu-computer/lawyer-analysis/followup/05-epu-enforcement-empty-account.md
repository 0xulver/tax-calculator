# Prompt 5 — Enforcing the EPU title when the company bank account is empty

## Role
You are a Polish enforcement specialist (egzekucja komornicza). Walk me through exactly what happens once I have a final, enforceable payment order, with a focus on the realistic scenario that the GPUcomputer bank account is empty and the debtor is abroad.

## Case facts
- Debtor: Mateusz Szklarski, sole proprietor (JDG) "GPUcomputer", NIP 8661681248, registered ul. Mogilska 16/7, Kraków (virtual office). A JDG = no corporate veil; he is personally liable with all assets.
- EPU claim Nc-e 552126/26 for 155,000 PLN + interest; I expect (hope) to obtain a final nakaz zapłaty with a clause of enforceability (klauzula wykonalności).
- Known account from public records: PL84 2490 0005 0000 4530 9127 6540 (likely Alior). Probably low/empty balance.
- The debtor lives and works full-time in the Netherlands (wages there); the Kraków business appears dormant/virtual.
- He is not known to own obvious Polish real estate; this needs verification.

## What I need

1. **Step-by-step enforcement procedure** once I hold the tytuł wykonawczy:
   - choosing/instructing a komornik, the wniosek egzekucyjny, and whether I can pick a komornik outside the debtor's district (art. 10 u.k.k. — prawo wyboru komornika) and why that might matter;
   - the upfront costs/advances I must pay and how komornik fees work (who ultimately bears them; the ~% taken on recovery);
   - timeline.

2. **What the komornik can search and seize in Poland**, and how:
   - **OGNIVO** bank-account search across all Polish banks;
   - **CEPiK** (vehicles), **land & mortgage registers / EKW** (real estate, hipoteka przymusowa);
   - ZUS/US data, receivables from third parties, cash, movables;
   - can the komornik attach the *business* assets and the *personal* assets of the JDG owner simultaneously (no subsidiarity)?

3. **If nothing is found in Poland** (empty account, no Polish real estate, income is in NL):
   - what is **umorzenie postępowania egzekucyjnego wobec bezskuteczności egzekucji** (art. 824 § 1 pkt 3 KPC), what triggers it, and what document/certificate I receive;
   - the **strategic value** of that "bezskuteczność" certificate — for tax write-off, for the criminal track (art. 300 KK / evidence of inability), for a later attempt, and for cross-border enforcement;
   - the **wyjawienie majątku** procedure (art. 913 KPC) — compelling the debtor to disclose assets under penalty; can it be used while he's abroad?
   - re-running enforcement later (the title doesn't expire quickly — confirm the limitation period for an enforceable title).

4. **Reaching the debtor's Dutch income from Poland** — at a high level only here (full treatment in prompt 6): does a Polish komornik have any reach over NL wages, or is a separate EU instrument mandatory?

5. **Anti-dissipation:** if I suspect he'll move assets once a nakaz issues, what's the fastest protective step available post-nakaz (given the lawyer says pre-judgment civil zabezpieczenie within EPU is not available)?

## Output format
A linear procedure walk-through, then a clearly separated "what if there's nothing in Poland" section with the concrete next moves and the value of the bezskuteczność certificate.
