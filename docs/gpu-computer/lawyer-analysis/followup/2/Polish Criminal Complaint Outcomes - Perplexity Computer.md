3. Money-recovery mechanisms inside the criminal process
Art. 46 § 1 KK — obowiązek naprawienia szkody (compensation order)
This is the mechanism that, on paper, hands you your 155k.

What it is: on conviction, the court — on the victim's motion, must (orzeka), or even of its own motion may (może) — order the offender to repair the damage in full or in part, applying civil-law principles.

Is it automatic on conviction? No — but your motion makes it obligatory. Without a victim's motion the court may impose it; with a properly filed art. 49a motion the court must (assuming the prerequisites and no art. 415 § 1 bar). So you file it. Always.

Timing of the art. 49a KPK motion: you can file it up to the close of the trial proceedings (zamknięcie przewodu sądowego) at the main hearing — orally to the protocol or in writing (Prokuratura Okręgowa w Kielcach; edukacja-prawna). In practice: file it early, in writing, and don't wait.

Is it an enforceable title on its own — no separate civil judgment? Yes — this is the key point. A criminal judgment's compensation order is a tytuł egzekucyjny under art. 777 § 1 pkt 3 KPC; once you get a klauzula wykonalności from the criminal court that issued it, you go straight to a komornik with an egzekucja application — exactly like a civil judgment, no separate civil suit needed (orzeczenia.lodz III Ca 194/20; edukacja-prawna). It becomes wymagalne on the date in the judgment, or — if none is set — on the date the judgment becomes final.

The catch: this title is identical in collectability to your civil EPU title. It's still only as good as Mateusz's reachable assets. And note art. 415 § 1 KPK anti-double-recovery: you cannot get a 46 KK order for the same claim already covered by a civil title — so the criminal track is in part an alternative route to a title, not a stack on top of the civil one. Practically: the criminal compensation order's advantage isn't a "second debt," it's that it can attach to assets the asset-freeze already locked down, and it costs you nothing to obtain because the prosecutor builds the case.

Art. 291–295 KPK — zabezpieczenie majątkowe (asset freeze)
This is the single most valuable thing the criminal route offers you, and the one the civil side reportedly can't give you.

When does it become available? Only once proceedings reach the in personam stage — i.e., once Mateusz is podejrzany (charges presented). It can only be applied to the accused's property (art. 291 § 1), so it is impossible before someone has been formally charged (Chmielniak adwokaci; Staniek&Partners). This is why item 3's "reach przedstawienie zarzutów" stage matters so much — everything protective unlocks there.

Two conditions, both required (łącznie): (1) in personam stage reached, and (2) a justified fear that without the freeze, executing the future judgment will be impossible or seriously hindered. Your fact pattern — debtor abroad, contradictory excuses, money apparently moved — builds condition (2) almost by itself.

What it can freeze: the accused's assets to cover anticipated fines, środki karne, przepadek, and crucially the art. 46 compensation — and, via art. 45 § 2–3 KK / konfiskata rozszerzona, even assets Mateusz has transferred to third parties to dodge it (Kancelaria Gatner; Staniek&Partners). The reach to transferred assets is something a vanilla civil zabezpieczenie does not have.

Who decides, and cost to you: in the investigation phase the prosecutor issues the freezing order (then submits it to the court for zatwierdzenie within 7 days; the court rules within 7 days); in the court phase the court does (UMCS materials; Kancelaria Gatner). Cost to you: effectively zero — you don't post security, you don't pay a deposit, the state acts. Contrast the civil zabezpieczenie, which typically demands you credibly show (uprawdopodobnić) both the claim and the legal interest, and can require security — the hurdle your lawyer apparently thinks you can't clear.

How it compares to civil zabezpieczenie: civil freeze can come earlier (you don't need anyone charged) but you must clear the interes prawny hurdle and possibly post security; criminal freeze comes later (needs a podejrzany) but is free, prosecutor-driven, and reaches hidden/transferred assets. They are not mutually exclusive — but the criminal freeze is the heavier instrument once it's available.

Restitution / mediation / voluntary repayment as a sentence-mitigation incentive
This is the most realistic path to actual cash, more so than running a 3-year case to verdict.

The legal lever: under art. 295 § 1 KK, an offender of fraud (it's explicitly listed) who voluntarily repairs the damage in full before the trial proceedings begin opens the door to nadzwyczajne złagodzenie kary — extraordinary mitigation — and even odstąpienie od wymierzenia kary (waiving punishment entirely); repairing it in significant part (§ 2) allows mitigation (UWM journal article; Kancelaria Klisz, citing art. 295/296). Plus, for a first-time offender at this loss level, courts most often impose suspended sentences, restriction of liberty, or fines — meaning the realistic downside Mateusz faces is a criminal record + a compensation order he'll have to pay anyway, which is exactly the bargaining structure that makes paying-to-make-it-go-away rational.

Does the threat realistically push a JDG to settle, and when? It depends entirely on solvency and on whether he wants to keep operating a business in Poland:

If solvent and rational: yes, meaningfully. A criminal record carries business consequences (and see your prompt 7 on non-dischargeability — a criminal-origin debt is far stickier in any future restructuring). The leverage spikes at exactly two moments: (a) the instant zarzuty are presented (suddenly real, suddenly his assets are frozen), and (b) just before the trial proceedings open, the art. 295 deadline, where full repayment buys him the best sentencing outcome available. That second window is the classic settlement point.

If insolvent or judgment-proof: the threat changes nothing about cash — you can't squeeze water from a stone, and a man willing to issue contradictory excuses across months may simply absorb a suspended sentence. Then the value is purely strategic (record, ban-pressure, non-dischargeability), not cash.
