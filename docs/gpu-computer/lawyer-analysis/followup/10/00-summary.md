# Research summary — The "fake front" misrepresentation theory

> All five outputs agree the fake-front theory is materially stronger than the bare "paid / no delivery / no refund" complaint, because it names a specific pre-payment *method* of deception — exactly what SN III KK 216/25 demands to get past the "civil dispute" screen. The lead count is the **insurance claim** ("Każda przesyłka… ubezpieczona do pełnej wartości"): specific, verifiable, not puffery, and now **proven live 33 days before payment** by a Wayback snapshot of `/sklep` dated 10.12.2025. One live-site fact forces a correction: the same banner says the shop is "TYLKO i WYŁĄCZNIE… wysyłkowy" (mail-order only), so any "fake physical store" framing must be dropped — the surviving M2 point is the virtual-office *seat* presented as a real one.

## Key findings (consensus)

- **Per-misrepresentation verdicts:**
  - **M3 (insurance) — lead count.** A binary, falsifiable assertion about a present operational practice. Either branch convicts the statement: if never shipped, the promise was structurally false; if "shipped and lost," the full-value insurance demonstrably didn't exist (no policy, no claim, no payout). The contradictory emails (24.02 "customs cleared" vs 13.05 "HK supplier bankrupt, parts never arrived") are the intent lever — they prevent the defense holding both stories.
  - **M1 (operating Kraków business / absentee owner) — spine of the intent and capacity narrative.** Deception about present capacity to perform (możliwość realizacji warunków transakcji), but largely *implied* rather than a quoted false sentence; works best in aggregate. Gap: official (not LinkedIn-only) proof of full-time NL employment at the contract date.
  - **M2 ("store" claim / seat) — corroboration only.** Demoted by the mail-order disclaimer; the residual point is presenting Mogilska 16/7 ("Our office" map pin) as a real operating seat when it is a virtual office (WorkDesk publicly advertises it as "Biuro wirtualne").
  - **Aggregate "fake front" — strongest overall.** The cumulation (insured-safety promise + operating-merchant appearance + concealed real operator) eliminates the "honest business failure" defense and is what proves *dolus directus coloratus* at contract formation.
- **Causation test:** "I would not have paid had I known" is necessary but is assessed against objective materiality — plead every misrepresentation as a fact objectively bearing on capacity/reliability to perform, not merely personal reassurance. Timing rule (SN III KK 100/17, ChatGPT 5.5): post-payment lies cannot be the *method*, only intent corroboration.
- **Puffery boundary:** "best workstations" = dolus bonus; "insured to full value" / "we have a store at X" = verifiable factual assertions → actionable. Analogy: Allegro non-existent-goods convictions.
- **Attribution:** name both men under art. 18 § 1 KK (Waldek = architect of the deceptive interface; Mateusz = the registered identity, NIP and account that received the 155k). **Firmanctwo (art. 55 KKS)** flagged as a related offence that corroborates the front and reaches Waldek — but don't conflate it with the fraud.
- **Likelihood:** concealment-only complaint ≈ >80% refusal risk; fake-front theory: screening survival substantially better (Gemini: 70–85% investigation opened), charges plausible-not-assured (Perplexity) to 60–75% (Gemini, optimistic outlier) — contingent on subpoena results (bank flows, supplier docs, policy existence, site-control logs).
