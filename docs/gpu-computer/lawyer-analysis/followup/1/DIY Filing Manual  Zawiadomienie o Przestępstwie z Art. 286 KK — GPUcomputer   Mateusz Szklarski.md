## 1. Gdzie Złożyć — Which Authority & Territorial Unit

### Primary Rule: Place of Offence (Art. 31 KPK)

Polish territorial jurisdiction in criminal matters is governed by **art. 31 § 1 KPK**: the competent prosecutor (or police) is the one in whose district (*okręg*) the offence was committed. The "place of offence" for a commercial fraud (art. 286 KK) is typically where the contract was performed — i.e., where the seller should have delivered the goods and where the buyer transferred the money.[^1]

**Key facts for your case:**

| Factor | Location |
|---|---|
| Seller's registered CEIDG address | ul. Mogilska 16/7, Kraków |
| Seller's actual residence | Netherlands |
| Buyer's residence | Warsaw |
| Payment origin | Buyer's bank account (Warsaw) |
| Place goods should be delivered | Warsaw (buyer's location) |

Because the fraudulent act (taking payment without ability/intent to deliver) was initiated from the Kraków virtual-office address registered in CEIDG, and the proforma invoice was issued under that business registration, Kraków is the natural primary jurisdiction. However, Warsaw (place of delivery failure, place where the financial damage was suffered) also constitutes a valid alternative basis.

### Subsidiary Rules (Art. 32 KPK)

If the primary place of offence is disputed, competence passes in sequence to: (a) where the offence was discovered/revealed, (b) where the suspect was apprehended, (c) where the suspect resides. Given that Mateusz physically resides in the Netherlands, rule (c) does not apply domestically.[^1]

## 2. Jak Złożyć z Zagranicy / Zdalnie

### Option A — Registered Post (List Polecony z Potwierdzeniem Odbioru) ✅ PREFERRED DIY METHOD

Sending by **registered post (list polecony za potwierdzeniem odbioru / list polecony za zwrotnym potwierdzeniem odbioru)** is fully valid. The KPK imposes no requirement to file in person. Art. 119 KPK specifies only the formal elements of a procesual document (designation of authority, parties, subject matter, date, signature) — no in-person appearance requirement.[^8][^9][^10]

**Practical steps:**
1. Print, sign with handwritten signature, scan for your records.
2. Mail from any Swedish/Polish post office as **list polecony z potwierdzeniem odbioru** (*doręczenie za zwrotnym potwierdzeniem*) to the Kraków address above.
3. Retain the postal receipt and keep the return confirmation card (ZPO) when received — this is your proof of filing date.
4. The filing date is the date the envelope was posted (equivalent to art. 124 KPK rule: date of delivery to postal operator counts as filing date).

**From Warsaw:** Since you reside in Warsaw (even as a foreign resident), you can simply go to any Polish post office (Poczta Polska) or private courier and mail to Kraków. Alternatively, deliver in person to any prokuratura in Warsaw and request it be forwarded.

### Option B — ePUAP / E-Prokuratura ✅ VIABLE (requires Profil Zaufany)

Each prokuratura in Poland has an official ePUAP skrytka (electronic inbox). Prokuratura Rejonowa Kraków–Śródmieście Wschód's ePUAP skrytka is: **PR_KrakowSrodmiescieWschod/SkrytkaESP**.[^8]

**Profil Zaufany for a Swedish citizen resident in Warsaw:**
- You can obtain a Profil Zaufany (PZ) even as a foreign national, provided you have a **PESEL number**.[^11]
- As a Warsaw resident you almost certainly already have a PESEL (required for Polish residence registration). If not, apply at any *urząd gminy* in Poland or by post.[^11]
- Once you have a PESEL, create an ePUAP account at **epuap.gov.pl** and confirm your Profil Zaufany through your Polish or Swedish e-banking (many major banks support this) — confirmation can be done entirely online without visiting a government office.[^12]
- Alternatively, use a **kwalifikowany podpis elektroniczny** (qualified electronic signature) — available from commercial providers (e.g., Certum, Sigillum) — which does not require a PESEL but costs approx. 200-350 PLN/year.

Documents sent via ePUAP must be signed with a PZ or qualified e-signature and should be in PDF format (max 5 MB per attachment).[^8]

⚠️ **DIY RISK — ePUAP technical issues:** ePUAP occasionally has uptime issues. Always keep a registered-post backup plan. If the electronic delivery fails, you will not have proof of timely filing.

### Option C — E-mail ❌ PROCEDURALLY FRAGILE

Sending by ordinary e-mail (even to the biuro.podawcze address) does **not** constitute valid filing under KPK. The prosecutor's office may acknowledge receipt but the document will not have the procedural status of a formal zawiadomienie until it is printed, registered, and assigned a reference number. Use only if you are in an emergency and follow up immediately with a registered-post original.

### Option D — Filing by a Proxy (Pełnomocnik) ✅ VALID

Any adult with legal capacity can file the zawiadomienie on your behalf, armed with a written **pełnomocnictwo** (power of attorney). The KPK (art. 88 and foll.) allows a pokrzywdzony to act through a representative in the pre-trial stage.[^13]

**Requirements for the pełnomocnictwo:**
- Simple private written document — notarisation is not required at the complaint stage (though recommended for certainty).[^13]
- Must specify: your name/data, the representative's name/data, and the scope ("do złożenia zawiadomienia o możliwości popełnienia przestępstwa i reprezentowania mnie jako pokrzywdzonego w postępowaniu przygotowawczym").
- The representative attaches the pełnomocnictwo original (or certified copy) to the zawiadomienie when filing.[^13]
- A Swedish-language pełnomocnictwo must be accompanied by a sworn Polish translation (*tłumaczenie przysięgłe*) at the filing stage if the document is not in Polish.

## 4. Teoria Oszustwa — Jak Sformułować Mechanizm Podstępu

### The SN III KK 216/25 Warning

The Supreme Court decision **III KK 216/25 (17.06.2025)** is the most important recent precedent you must navigate. The SN held unequivocally:[^20][^21][^22]

> *"Sam fakt pobrania zaliczki na poczet wykonania umowy, a następnie brak realizacji zobowiązania i nie zwrócenie pobranej kwoty stanowi jedynie podstawę odpowiedzialności kontraktowej, nie zaś przestępstwo oszustwa."*[^22]

**The consequence:** "took prepayment, didn't deliver, didn't refund" standing alone = civil matter, not crime. Your complaint will be dismissed at the postępowanie sprawdzające stage if it merely recites this fact pattern without articulating a specific deception method.[^21][^20]

### The Three Statutory Deception Methods (Art. 286 § 1 KK)

The Act requires one of three mechanisms:[^22]
1. **Wprowadzenie w błąd** — actively inducing a false belief
2. **Wyzyskanie błędu** — exploiting an existing error
3. **Wyzyskanie niezdolności do należytego pojmowania** — exploiting incapacity

Your case rests primarily on **introducing into error (wprowadzenie w błąd)**, with a powerful secondary argument of **concealment of material risk (przemilczenie)**.

### The Deception Theory to Plead: Four Specific Arguments

**Argument 1 — Concealment of Inability/Non-Existence of Supply Chain (Przemilczenie)**

The seller accepted 155,000 PLN prepayment while concealing the material fact that: (a) he had no confirmed supply chain for the components, (b) the supposed Hong Kong supplier had not yet delivered or confirmed the order. The obligation to disclose arose because the transaction was structured as full prepayment — a buyer paying 100% upfront reasonably relies on the seller's representation (express or implied) that the goods are available or firmly sourced. Deliberate silence about a fundamental obstacle to performance constitutes *wprowadzenie w błąd przez przemilczenie* (fraud by omission), recognised in Polish jurisprudence as sufficient for art. 286 KK.

**Wording:** *"Podejrzany, przyjmując zaliczkę w kwocie 155 000 PLN, zatajał przed pokrzywdzonym — wbrew obowiązkowi ujawnienia istotnych okoliczności transakcji — że nie posiadał pewnego łańcucha dostaw ani potwierdzonych zamówień u dostawcy zagranicznego, co stanowiło przesłankę niezbywalną dla podjęcia przez pokrzywdzonego decyzji o zapłacie całej ceny z góry. Przemilczenie tej okoliczności wytworzyło u pokrzywdzonego błędne wyobrażenie, że GPUcomputer dysponuje możliwością wykonania zamówienia."*

**Argument 2 — Contradictory Explanations Proving Ex Ante Bad Faith**

The seller gave two **mutually exclusive** explanations: (i) on 24.02.2026: "customs cleared today — missing components arriving soon" — implying goods were ordered and in transit; (ii) on 13.05.2026: "Hong Kong supplier went bankrupt and paid-for components never arrived" — implying the seller himself had paid a supplier and that supplier failed. These stories cannot both be true. The existence of contradictory post-hoc explanations is strong circumstantial evidence that at the time of taking payment (January 2026), neither story was true — i.e., the seller had not in fact placed confirmed orders with any supplier. This supports the inference of **dolus directus coloratus** (specific intent to deceive and profit) existing at the time of contract.

**Wording:** *"Sprzeczność wyjaśnień (w lutym twierdzono, że towar przeszedł odprawę celną; w maju — że dostawca nigdy nie dostarczył opłaconych podzespołów) wskazuje, że żadna z tych wersji nie odpowiada prawdzie i że w chwili przyjęcia zapłaty podejrzany nie miał rzeczywistego tytułu do obiecania dostawy. Sprzeczność ta stanowi dowód pośredni istniejącego już w dacie zawarcia umowy zamiaru doprowadzenia pokrzywdzonego do niekorzystnego rozporządzenia mieniem."*

**Argument 3 — Virtual Office / No Real Business Infrastructure**

The business address is a **virtual office** (wirtualne biuro) — it has no physical premises, no warehouse, no staff capable of assembling an ML workstation worth 155,000 PLN. The actual operations were conducted remotely from the Netherlands. This pattern — a JDG registered at a virtual office, accepting large prepayments for high-value custom hardware, operated by a principal residing abroad — is structurally consistent with a pattern of fraudulent intent (inability to perform was built into the business model from the outset).

**Wording:** *"Fakt prowadzenia działalności pod adresem wirtualnego biura, przy jednoczesnym faktycznym pobycie podejrzanego za granicą i braku jakiejkolwiek infrastruktury technicznej umożliwiającej realizację zamówień na specjalistyczny sprzęt komputerowy o wartości ponad 150 000 PLN, wskazuje na pozorność tej działalności i planowe doprowadzanie kontrahentów do niekorzystnego rozporządzenia mieniem."*

**Argument 4 — Simultaneous Conduct by Waldemar Łopata (Two Mailboxes, One Minute Apart)**

Both mailboxes sent the written repayment promise within one minute of each other (10.03.2026). This indicates coordinated conduct and that Waldemar Łopata was not merely an employee but a co-actor — potentially constituting **współsprawstwo** (co-perpetration, art. 18 § 1 KK) or at minimum **pomocnictwo** (aiding, art. 18 § 3 KK). Pleading co-perpetration strengthens the case because it reduces the argument that one person acted without the other's knowledge.

### ⚠️ DIY RISK — Dolus Directus

The hardest element to establish is that the seller had fraudulent intent **at the moment of contract** (ex ante), not merely that he later failed to perform. Without internal documents, bank records, or prior victim statements showing a pattern, circumstantial arguments (virtual office, contradictory stories) may not satisfy a cautious prosecutor. This is the point where a lawyer adds the most value — experienced practitioners know which patterns of circumstantial evidence consistently persuade Kraków prosecutors.

### Do English-Language Documents Need Sworn Translations at Complaint Stage?

**No — sworn translations (tłumaczenia przysięgłe) are NOT required at the zawiadomienie stage.**

The KPK does not impose a sworn-translation requirement for exhibits attached to a zawiadomienie. The prosecutor's office will request translations if and when proceedings are formally opened. Plain (non-sworn) working translations attached to the English originals are courteous and will speed processing, but they are not a formal prerequisite.[^23][^24]

**When sworn translations become required:**
- After formal opening of dochodzenie/śledztwo, when documents become evidence (*dowody*) in the proceeding.
- If the prosecutor requests them as part of postępowanie sprawdzające under art. 307 KPK.[^25]
- At the trial stage, definitely.

**Cost guidance (2025–2026):**
- Sworn translation: approx. 50–120 PLN per standard A4 page, depending on complexity and provider.[^23]
- Typical evidence package for this case: ~20–40 pages of English documents → approx. 1,000–4,800 PLN.
- Non-sworn working translation: 30–70 PLN/page.

### Document Volume

Expect 30–60 pages total for the initial filing package (complaint + translations + exhibits). This is a manageable self-preparation task.

## 6. Co Się Dzieje Po Złożeniu — Procedural Path After Filing

### Stage 1: Postępowanie Sprawdzające (Art. 307 KPK)

Upon receiving the zawiadomienie, the prosecutor may (but need not) conduct a **verification/screening procedure** before deciding whether to open formal proceedings. During this stage:[^26][^25]

- The prosecutor may request supplementary information (*uzupełnienie danych*) within a set deadline.
- No expert opinions may be commissioned; no full interrogations of suspects take place.[^26]
- **Deadline:** If postępowanie sprawdzające is opened, the prosecutor must issue a decision (to open or refuse to open proceedings) within **30 days** from receipt of the zawiadomienie (art. 307 § 1 KPK).[^25]

### Stage 2: Notification Obligation and the 6-Week Rule

Under **art. 306 § 3 KPK**, if the complainant has not been notified of either the opening or the refusal to open proceedings within **6 weeks** of filing the zawiadomienie, the complainant may lodge a **zażalenie** (complaint/appeal) to the *prokurator nadrzędny* or the supervisory body. This is a safeguard against inaction — use it promptly if 6 weeks pass without any response.[^27][^28]

### Stage 3: Opening Formal Proceedings

If the prosecutor issues a **postanowienie o wszczęciu dochodzenia** (or śledztwa), you will be notified under art. 305 § 4 KPK. As the pokrzywdzony, you gain formal party status and can review the file, submit additional evidence, and be heard.[^17]

**Dochodzenie vs. Śledztwo:**
- For art. 286 § 1 KK at 155,000 PLN (below the 200,000 PLN threshold for art. 294), a **dochodzenie** (conducted primarily by Police under prosecutorial supervision) is the default.[^7]
- You may request (in the zawiadomienie or separately) that the prosecutor convert it to a **śledztwo** due to complexity — cross-border elements, two suspects, large value, international judicial assistance needed (art. 309 § 5 KPK).[^7]

### Stage 4: Refusal to Open Proceedings — Odmowa Wszczęcia

If the prosecutor issues a **postanowienie o odmowie wszczęcia dochodzenia**, you receive a copy by post and are told your rights.[^29]

### Stage 5: Appeal — Zażalenie (Art. 306 KPK)

**Deadline:** **7 days** from the date of receipt (*doręczenia*) of the postanowienie — this is a strict preclusive deadline; late filing is automatically ineffective.[^30][^31][^29]

**Addressee and routing:** The zażalenie is addressed to the **Sąd Rejonowy właściwy dla miejsca prowadzenia sprawy** (i.e., the Sąd Rejonowy for Kraków–Śródmieście) but is submitted **through (za pośrednictwem) the prosecutor who issued the refusal**.[^31][^30]

**Content:** The zażalenie must identify specific errors in the prosecutor's decision — e.g., failure to consider the deception evidence, incorrect classification as civil dispute, omission of key documentary evidence. A generic "I disagree" will be overruled.[^31]

⚠️ **DIY RISK — Zażalenie:** Drafting an effective zażalenie requires analysing the prosecutor's legal reasoning in the postanowienie. This is genuinely difficult without legal experience. The 7-day deadline also creates pressure. This is the step where engaging a lawyer provides the clearest value — a poorly reasoned zażalenie will be dismissed and closes the primary appeal path.

## 8. Consolidated DIY Risk Assessment

| Step | DIY Risk Level | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Choosing the right prokuratura | 🟡 Medium | Territorial ambiguity; filing in Warsaw causes transfer delay |
| Drafting the opis czynu / deception theory | 🔴 High | SN III KK 216/25 makes this the critical filter; weak framing = dismissal |
| Uzasadnienie (detailed factual narrative) | 🟡 Medium | Needs to be specific and documentary; manageable if you organise evidence well |
| Translations at filing stage | 🟢 Low | Not legally required at complaint stage |
| Registered post filing | 🟢 Low | Straightforward; just keep postal receipt |
| ePUAP filing | 🟡 Medium | Requires Profil Zaufany; technical issues possible |
| Filing via proxy with pełnomocnictwo | 🟢 Low | Valid and simple; use trusted Warsaw contact |
| Responding to prosecutor's supplement request | 🟡 Medium | Requires quick, precise written response within set deadline |
| Zażalenie on odmowa wszczęcia | 🔴 High | 7-day strict deadline; must engage with legal reasoning; highest value point for a lawyer |
