# Research summary — How to file the zawiadomienie myself

> **Bottom line:** All five models agree this is fully doable yourself, for free, addressed to **Prokuratura Rejonowa Kraków-Śródmieście Wschód, ul. Mosiężnicza 2, 30-965 Kraków**. **Filing channel for Magnus specifically: ePUAP "Pismo ogólne", signed with his existing Profil Zaufany** — the models recommended registered post only as a fallback *for someone who lacks* a Profil Zaufany; that caveat does not apply here (see "How" below). The make-or-break element is not procedure but framing: because of SN III KK 216/25, a complaint that says only "I paid, no delivery, no refund" will be screened out as a civil dispute. You must plead a *method of deception* — concealment (przemilczenie) of the missing supply chain at the moment of payment, with the contradictory "customs cleared" (24.02) vs "Hong Kong supplier bankrupt" (13.05) stories as circumstantial proof of intent. The two points where a lawyer adds real value are that framing and any 7-day zażalenie after a refusal.

## Key findings (consensus across models)

- **Where:** Prokuratura (not police) — PR Kraków-Śródmieście Wschód covers Mogilska 16/7 (Dzielnica II Grzegórzki). Filing in Warsaw is legally valid (art. 118 § 3 KPK transfer) but costs weeks; file directly in Kraków.
- **How (digital — the route for Magnus):** ePUAP **"Wyślij pismo ogólne"** to the prosecutor's electronic inbox (skrytka ESP; the DIY Manual gives `PR_KrakowSrodmiescieWschod/SkrytkaESP` — confirm on their gov.pl page), with the PDF complaint **signed by Profil Zaufany** (legally equal to a handwritten signature). You receive a **UPP/UPO** electronic receipt = proof of filing, equivalent to a postal return receipt. This is *not* the e-Doręczenia system — for justice institutions (prosecutor/police/courts) the route is ePUAP/pismo ogólne. Two frictions: (1) **attachment size limit** — models cite 3 MB total in XAdES-BES (Prokuratura Krajowa / ChatGPT 5.5) vs 5 MB per attachment (DIY Manual), so the exhibit bundle likely needs splitting across several pisma or PDF compression; (2) ePUAP occasional downtime — fine for the deadline-free initial complaint, but mind it for the 7-day zażalenie clock later.
- **How (postal fallback):** registered post with return receipt (list polecony za potwierdzeniem odbioru), original with **handwritten ink signature**, keep the postal receipt. Models recommended this *only* to avoid forcing someone without a Profil Zaufany to build digital identity first. Filing is free either way. Plain email alone (unsigned) is procedurally fragile/ineffective — use it only to follow up, never as the sole filing.
- **Language/form:** Complaint itself in Polish (all five provide a template). Sworn translations of English exhibits are **not** required at filing (art. 204 § 2 KPK puts that on the authority); attach your own working Polish translations of the key emails, quote decisive lines in-body. Attach copies, never originals; offer originals/.eml files on request.
- **Content:** art. 119 KPK skeleton — authority, your data as pokrzywdzony (art. 49 KPK) with Polish service address, suspects (Szklarski + Łopata, "roles to be established"), precise opis czynu, qualification art. 286 § 1 KK (155k is below the 200k art. 294 threshold → dochodzenie), uzasadnienie, wnioski dowodowe (bank flows, mailbox/IP logs, customs records, interrogations), art. 46 § 1 KK compensation request. Plead "uzasadnione podejrzenie", never assert guilt (art. 234 KK). Plead współsprawstwo (art. 18 § 1 KK) via the one-minute-apart refund promises. Disclose the EPU case proactively and explain why the criminal track is distinct.
- **Deadlines:** ~30-day screening decision (art. 307); **6 weeks** without notification → complaint about inaction to the superior prosecutor (art. 306 § 3); refusal → **zażalenie within 7 days of service** (preclusive), filed *through* the refusing prosecutor, decided by Sąd Rejonowy dla Krakowa-Śródmieścia (ul. Przy Rondzie 7).

## Where the models disagree or add unique points

- **Friend with POA:** the DIY Filing Manual says any adult can file with a simple POA; ChatGPT 5.5 Pro, ChatGPT Deep Research and Gemini say a true criminal pełnomocnik must be an adwokat/radca (art. 88 KPK) — a friend can only deliver papers or serve as address-for-service; Kimi treats POA as viable but says a Swedish-signed POA needs notarisation + apostille + sworn translation (~240–540 PLN).
- **Signature strictness:** Kimi (SN I KZP 29/06, SA Kraków II AKz 752/24) and Gemini insist scanned/printed signatures are void — ink only.
- **Gemini:** warns against oral police filing in Warsaw (poor protocol), stresses the art. 138 KPK awizo trap, and recommends sworn-translating the 3–5 key emails before filing (~200–400 PLN).
- **Kimi:** 30-day screening deadline is only instructive; request an art. 304b filing confirmation; zażalenie lawyer costs 3,000–8,000 PLN; explicit staged strategy "DIY the complaint, hire counsel immediately upon refusal"; extra precedent IV KK 403/24 (concealment).
- **ChatGPT Deep Research:** gives a police alternative (Komisariat II, ul. Lubicz 21) and official translation tariff (44.07 PLN/page EN→PL).

## Action checklist

1. Adapt one Polish template (cross-check facts/dates against your documents).
2. Lead with the concealment theory + contradictory-explanations argument; keep all language conditional ("może wyczerpywać znamiona"). *(See folder 10: the fake-front affirmative-misrepresentation theory is now the stronger lead, with concealment folded in.)*
3. Assemble annexes as copies: proforma 2/01-2026, both payment confirmations, key emails with full headers (24.02, 02.03, both 10.03, 13.05), CEIDG, KRZ printout, EPU confirmation; add your own signed working translations of the key emails.
4. Export the complaint as PDF, **sign it with Profil Zaufany, and send via ePUAP "Pismo ogólne"** to the prosecutor's skrytka; save the UPP/UPO receipt. (Split or compress the exhibit bundle to fit the size limit.) Give a Warsaw service address. Postal registered-mail is the fallback only if ePUAP fails.
5. Calendar the 6-week mark; chase inaction with the superior prosecutor if silent.
6. Watch for the decision (ePUAP inbox + mail); if refused, get the file (art. 306 § 1b) and engage a lawyer for the 7-day zażalenie immediately.

## Caveats / open questions

- Confirm the exact district unit by phone before mailing (Mogilska straddles districts; PR Kraków-Krowodrza sits at Mogilska 17 but does *not* cover Grzegórzki).
- Minor factual inconsistencies across drafts (Gemini assumes a 02.01.2026 proforma date; Kimi shows 09.01/17.01 transfer dates in one section) — verify every date against your records before filing.
- None of this is legal advice; the models themselves flag the deception-theory section and the zażalenie as the points where paid counsel is cheapest insurance.
