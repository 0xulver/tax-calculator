Zawiadomienie o podejrzeniu popełnienia przestępstwa — a DIY filing manual
1. Where to file
Authority: Prokuratura Rejonowa, not the Policja komisariat. Legally either will accept it — every police officer is obliged to receive a zawiadomienie. But for an economic-fraud case at 155,000 PLN with a contested civil/criminal boundary, file directly with the prosecutor. The police would only forward it to the prosecutor anyway, and a self-drafted document going straight to a prosecutor avoids a police "protokół ustnego zawiadomienia" that may flatten your carefully-built deception theory into a thin summary.

Territorial competence (właściwość miejscowa). The governing rule is the place where the offence was committed (miejsce popełnienia czynu) — under the Regulamin prokuratorski, the prosecutor competent is the one in whose district the offence was committed, and only if that cannot be determined does the place where the zawiadomienie was received govern. For oszustwo, the offence is "committed" both where the deceptive conduct occurred and where the disposition of property/result occurred — which gives you a genuine choice of hooks: the seller's registered business seat in Kraków, and arguably your account/residence in Warsaw.

The likely competent Kraków unit. The CEIDG seat is ul. Mogilska 16/7, Kraków. Mogilska 16 sits at the western end of the street, in Dzielnica II Grzegórzki. Grzegórzki (Dzielnica pomocnicza II) falls under:

2. How to file from abroad / remotely
You have four channels. Ranked by reliability for your situation:

(a) Registered post (list polecony za zwrotnym potwierdzeniem odbioru) — RECOMMENDED. Fully valid. A written zawiadomienie meeting art. 119 KPK pismo-procesowe requirements can be mailed in. Send it "za zwrotnym potwierdzeniem odbioru" so you get a dated delivery confirmation — this stamps the start of the 6-week clock (§6). You can post from the Netherlands/Sweden/anywhere; address it to ul. Mosiężnicza 2. Keep the nadanie receipt and the ZPO card.

(b) ePUAP / e-Doręczenia — valid but requires Polish credentials. Prosecutor's offices accept electronic documents via the ePUAP ESP skrytka, either by completing a form or attaching a signed document online; Śródmieście-Wschód lists ESP as a channel for some matters. To sign you need a Profil Zaufany or a qualified e-signature. As a Swedish citizen resident in Warsaw you can obtain a Profil Zaufany, but it requires: a PESEL number, a valid ID (passport/karta pobytu), and a Polish mobile number for the SMS codes. Since you live in Warsaw you likely already have a PESEL and Polish SIM, so this is feasible — but it's setup overhead. Note: an EU qualified e-signature also satisfies it.

(c) E-mail — procedurally fragile, avoid as the sole channel. Sending a written zawiadomienie by e-mail is tolerated in practice, but a plain unsigned e-mail does not satisfy the pismo-procesowe signature requirement and the office may treat it as merely a signal requiring you to re-file properly. Use e-mail only to follow up on a posted complaint, never as the primary filing.

(d) Polish-resident friend filing on your behalf with a power of attorney — usable but with a limit. Anyone can file a zawiadomienie — the social duty to report (art. 304 § 1 KPK) means the informer need not be the victim. So a friend can physically lodge or post it. But the procedural rights of the pokrzywdzony (status as injured party, the right to be notified, the right to zażalenie) attach to you, Magnus, not the friend. A friend can act as your pełnomocnik if you grant a written pełnomocnictwo, but a pełnomocnik who is not an adwokat/radca prawny has limited standing in some procedural acts. Recommendation: sign the complaint yourself (scan/wet-ink), name yourself as pokrzywdzony with a Polish correspondence address, and optionally have a friend hand-deliver it with a simple pełnomocnictwo do doręczeń (service agent) so notices reach you fast.

⚠️ The hidden DIY cost here: if you file by post from abroad with only a foreign address, official decisions (esp. a postanowienie o odmowie wszczęcia, which must be served, not just mailed informally) can take much longer to reach you — and your 7-day appeal clock runs from service. Always give a Polish correspondence address (your Warsaw address or a friend acting as adres do doręczeń).

Your job is to hand the prosecutor a ready-made theory of deception and intent, not just non-performance.

4. The deception theory to plead (the core of your case)
This is decisive. The SN line is firmly against you on the bare facts: III KK 216/25 (17.06.2025) held — on RPO's cassation — that taking an advance, failing to perform, and failing to refund is contractual liability, not fraud, because non-performance is not equivalent to the statutory element of "wprowadzenie w błąd" (inducing error);;. Art. 286 § 1 requires dolus directus coloratus — direct, purposeful intent existing at the moment of taking the money.

So you must NOT plead "they didn't deliver and didn't refund." You must plead a method of deception and facts pointing to intent at the time of payment (12.01.2026). Your strongest hooks:

(a) Concealment of inability to perform / supplier risk (przemilczenie istotnych okoliczności). Case law expressly recognises that if the actor knew his situation would not allow performance and consciously concealed it from the buyer, that is fraud under art. 286 § 1. Plead: at the moment of issuing proforma 2/01-2026 and accepting 155,000 PLN, the sellers concealed that the goods depended on an unsecured third-party supply chain whose collapse they later invoked — a material fact which, had it been disclosed, would have stopped you from paying (the Szczecin "had I known the true state I would not have contracted" standard — adwokatdulniak.pl).

(b) The mutually contradictory explanations — your single best intent indicator. This is what lifts your case above III KK 216/25. On 24.02.2026 they wrote "customs cleared the goods today, missing components will arrive" — asserting the goods existed and were in customs. On 13.05.2026 they wrote "a Hong Kong supplier went bankrupt and the paid-for components never arrived" — asserting the goods never existed. Both cannot be true. Plead this expressly: the irreconcilable accounts demonstrate that at least one was a knowingly false statement made to maintain the deception and forestall recovery, which evidences that the original representation of ability to deliver was itself false — i.e., deception existing from the outset, not a supervening misfortune.

(c) The coordinated written repayment promise as consciousness/continuation, not exculpation. The 10.03.2026 promises from both mailboxes one minute apart, then total non-payment, show coordinated conduct and reinforce that the contradictory stories were managed messaging. (Be careful: a refund promise alone tends to look civil; deploy it as evidence of coordination, not as the gravamen.)

„Z góry powzięty zamiar oraz wprowadzenie pokrzywdzonego w błąd wynikają nie z samego niewykonania umowy, lecz ze sposobu działania zawiadamianych: w chwili wystawienia proformy nr 2/01-2026 i przyjęcia kwoty 155 000 zł zataili oni (przemilczeli) istotną okoliczność braku zabezpieczonego źródła dostaw i realnej zdolności do wykonania zamówienia, a następnie przedstawiali wzajemnie sprzeczne, wykluczające się wersje zdarzeń (24.02.2026 — towar 'odprawiony przez urząd celny'; 13.05.2026 — komponenty 'nigdy nie dotarły' wskutek upadłości dostawcy z Hongkongu), co — w mojej ocenie — wskazuje, że co najmniej jedno z tych oświadczeń było świadomie nieprawdziwe i służyło podtrzymaniu błędu pokrzywdzonego oraz udaremnieniu odzyskania środków.”

6. What happens after filing
Intake & screening (art. 307 KPK). The prosecutor may conduct czynności sprawdzające — limited, mostly un-recorded acts (the one exception: a victim may be heard as a witness). Within 30 days of receiving the zawiadomienie, the prosecutor must issue a postanowienie either o wszczęciu (open) or o odmowie wszczęcia (refuse).

How you learn the outcome. If they open, the matter proceeds as dochodzenie (likely here — art. 325b; oszustwo at this value is typically dochodzenie unless complexity pushes it to śledztwo). A refusal (postanowienie o odmowie wszczęcia) must be served on you as pokrzywdzony, with reasons.

The 6-week notification rule (art. 306 § 3 KPK). If within 6 weeks of the authority receiving your zawiadomienie you are not notified whether an investigation was opened or refused, you may file a zażalenie na bezczynność to the superior prosecutor (prokurator nadrzędny);. The 6 weeks = 30 days to decide + ~2 weeks to notify. This is your tool against silence — calendar it from the ZPO delivery date.

Appealing a refusal — zażalenie (art. 306 § 1 KPK). ✅ Confirmed: 7 days from service of the postanowienie o odmowie wszczęcia (art. 460 KPK), filed in writing, via the prosecutor who issued it (za pośrednictwem prokuratora). ✅ Court route confirmed: the zażalenie is heard by the sąd rejonowy competent for the case — a special rule under art. 306 § 2 KPK that overrides the general art. 465 § 2 KPK rule (which would send it to the superior prosecutor);. If you appear personally (not via lawyer) you needn't cite the exact provisions violated, but you must describe concretely what was done wrong or omitted.

⚠️ DIY false-economy flag — the appeal. The 7-day clock is short and unforgiving, runs from service (hence the Polish correspondence address point in §2), and a successful zażalenie must attack the prosecutor's reasoning — typically the very SN "this is civil" reasoning. Drafting an effective zażalenie that gets a sąd rejonowy to overturn a refusal is the second place (after §4) where a lawyer materially lowers dismissal risk. If your initial complaint is refused, strongly consider counsel for the appeal.
