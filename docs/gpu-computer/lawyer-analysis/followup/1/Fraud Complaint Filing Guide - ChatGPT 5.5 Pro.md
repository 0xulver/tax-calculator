## 2. How to file from abroad or remotely

### 2.1 Registered post

A paper complaint signed by you and sent by **list polecony** is the safest DIY method. From an EU country, KPK art. 124 preserves procedural deadlines if the letter is posted before expiry through a correspondence-delivery operator in the EU or at a Polish consulate. This matters especially for later appeals; for the initial complaint there is no short filing deadline, but the proof of posting still matters. ([Isap][4])

Use: **list polecony / registered tracked letter**, preferably with return receipt. Keep the posting receipt and a PDF scan of the entire packet.

### 2.2 ePUAP / electronic filing

Electronic filing is possible only if the document is properly signed. KPK art. 119 § 3 refers to qualified electronic signature, trusted signature, or personal signature for portal filings; gov.pl states that Profil Zaufany allows you to sign documents electronically with the same weight as a handwritten signature. ([Isap][4])

In practice, prosecution ePUAP pages are stricter and technically awkward. Prokuratura Krajowa’s ePUAP page says documents must be signed with a valid qualified digital signature in XAdES-BES format and that all attachments in one electronic form cannot exceed 3 MB; the Kraków prosecution ePUAP page gives the same 3 MB limit and qualified-signature wording. ([Gov.pl][5])

For a foreigner resident in Poland, Profil Zaufany normally depends on having a PESEL. Gov.pl states that a foreigner who registers residence in Poland for more than 30 days receives PESEL automatically; if registration is not possible and an office requires PESEL, the foreigner may apply at any gmina office. ([Gov.pl][6])

**Practical conclusion:** for this case, use registered post as the main filing route. Use ePUAP only as a backup if you already have a working ePUAP/Profil Zaufany or qualified signature and can keep the UPO. Do not rely on ePUAP for a thick exhibit bundle unless you split attachments carefully.

### 2.3 E-mail

Do **not** rely on e-mail alone. Gov.pl’s general crime-reporting page says a written report may be sent by e-mail, fax, or post and that you may later receive a summons to supplement it orally. However, Prokuratura Krajowa’s own procedural-filing page states expressly that an e-mail inbox is not for sending criminal-procedure filings. ([Gov.pl][2])

**Practical conclusion:** e-mail may alert the office, but it is procedurally fragile. If you send anything by e-mail, also send the signed original by post or ePUAP.

### 2.4 Filing through a Polish-resident friend

A friend can physically post or hand-deliver your **already signed** complaint. Gov.pl says that someone else may help send a notice to Police or prosecution. ([Gov.pl][2])

A friend cannot act as your KPK procedural representative merely on a private power of attorney. Under KPK art. 87–88, a party other than the accused may appoint a representative, but the representative must be an advocate, legal adviser, or Prokuratoria lawyer. ([Isap][4])

A friend can, however, be named as an **adresat do doręczeń** if you need a reliable Polish/EU service address. If you are outside Poland and outside the EU, KPK art. 138 requires you to indicate an address for service in Poland or another EU member state; otherwise letters may be deemed served by being sent to the last known address or placed in the file. ([Isap][4])

**DIY dismissal risk:** using a friend to sign, argue, inspect files, or file appeals is a procedural trap. Use a friend only as messenger or service recipient.

## 3. What the complaint must contain

A criminal-procedure filing must contain: the authority, the case it concerns, your name/address and contact details, the request or statement with reasons where needed, date, and signature. If formal defects prevent the filing from moving forward, the authority calls on you to cure them within 7 days; if cured, the filing is effective from the original filing date. ([Isap][4])

Your status is **pokrzywdzony**, because KPK art. 49 § 1 defines a victim as a person whose legal interest was directly violated or threatened by the offence. ([Isap][4])

The threshold is not proof beyond reasonable doubt. KPK art. 303 uses **uzasadnione podejrzenie popełnienia przestępstwa** as the threshold for opening proceedings. KPK art. 307 allows a preliminary verification stage: the authority may ask for supplementation or check facts, and if it uses that path, the decision to open or refuse must be issued within 30 days of receiving the notice. ([Isap][4])

Your document should have these sections:

1. **Oznaczenie organu** — PR Kraków-Śródmieście Wschód, or Warsaw unit with transfer request.
2. **Dane pokrzywdzonego** — full name, citizenship, address for service, phone, e-mail, passport/PESEL if you have it.
3. **Osoby, których dotyczy zawiadomienie** — Mateusz Szklarski / Szklarski-Łopata, GPUcomputer, NIP, CEIDG address, and Waldemar Łopata as the person using/controlling e-mail accounts, plus “inne osoby ustalone w toku postępowania.”
4. **Precyzyjny opis czynu** — dates, amount, invoice, payment route, no delivery, no refund, contradictory explanations, refund promise, civil EPU claim, no KRZ insolvency.
5. **Kwalifikacja prawna** — art. 286 § 1 KK, possibly “ewentualnie inne ujawnione w toku postępowania przepisy.”
6. **Uzasadnienie** — focus on deception at or before payment, not merely breach after payment.
7. **Wnioski dowodowe** — bank records, mailbox logs, e-mail headers, supplier/customs documents, witness questioning, CEIDG/KRZ/EPU documents.
8. **Wniosek o ściganie** — legally unnecessary for ordinary art. 286 § 1 unless the act was against a closest person, but include a belt-and-braces request.
9. **Wniosek o potwierdzenie złożenia zawiadomienia** — KPK art. 304b gives the victim the right to receive confirmation of filing with core case details. ([Isap][4])

Use the language “**zawiadamiam o uzasadnionym podejrzeniu**” and “**osoby, których dotyczy zawiadomienie**,” not “they are criminals.” KK art. 234 penalizes knowingly false accusation, and KK art. 238 penalizes knowingly notifying about an offence that was not committed. Accurate facts plus cautious inferences are the safe posture. ([Isap][3])

**DIY dismissal risk:** the highest-risk drafting mistake is to describe only non-performance and non-refund. The complaint must plead the suspected false picture created before you transferred PLN 155,000.

## 4. The deception theory to plead

Art. 286 § 1 KK requires that, for material gain, a person caused another to make an unfavourable disposal of property by misleading them, exploiting their error, or exploiting incapacity. The offence is not just failure to perform a contract. ([Isap][3])

The Supreme Court judgment **III KK 216/25 of 17 June 2025** is directly relevant. The Court stated that fraud under art. 286 § 1 KK requires one of the statutory deception methods and that the victim’s false view of reality must concern facts material to the property decision. It also held that merely taking an advance payment, not performing, and not refunding can be only contractual liability, not automatically fraud. ([Supreme Court][7])

So do **not** frame the case as: “I paid; they did not deliver; they did not refund.” Frame it as: “I paid because they created or maintained a materially false picture of their ability, control, supplier situation, customs status, and intention to perform/refund.”

Concrete wording to use:

> Nie zarzucam wyłącznie niewykonania umowy cywilnej. Uzasadnione podejrzenie dotyczy tego, że jeszcze przed przyjęciem płatności oraz w chwili jej przyjmowania osoby działające za GPUcomputer wytworzyły u mnie niezgodny z rzeczywistością obraz, że firma ma realną, kontrolowaną i zabezpieczoną możliwość nabycia oraz dostarczenia zamówionej stacji roboczej ML, ewentualnie przemilczały znane im istotne okoliczności: zależność wykonania od niezweryfikowanego lub niewypłacalnego dostawcy, brak faktycznie dostępnych komponentów, brak rzeczywistej odprawy celnej, brak środków lub zamiaru ich przeznaczenia na wykonanie zamówienia albo zwrot ceny.

Use the contradictions as evidence of suspected deception, not as proof by themselves:

> Wiadomość z 24.02.2026 r., że „customs cleared the goods today” i że brakujące komponenty dotrą, pozostaje w oczywistej sprzeczności z późniejszą wiadomością z 13.05.2026 r., że dostawca z Hongkongu zbankrutował, a opłacone komponenty nigdy nie dotarły. Obie wersje wymagają weryfikacji przez dokumenty dostawcy, potwierdzenia płatności, listy przewozowe/AWB, zgłoszenia celne i dokumenty odprawy. Sprzeczność ta uzasadnia podejrzenie, że wcześniejsze zapewnienia mogły być nieprawdziwe już w dacie przyjęcia środków.

Tie the refund promise to consciousness and coordination:

> Dwie wiadomości z 10.03.2026 r., wysłane z dwóch skrzynek gpucomputer.pl w odstępie jednej minuty, zawierały jednolitą obietnicę zwrotu 155 000 zł z odsetkami w terminie 14 dni roboczych. Brak jakiejkolwiek płatności po upływie terminu, przy wcześniejszym oferowaniu pełnego zwrotu, uzasadnia potrzebę ustalenia, czy obietnica zwrotu była realna, czy służyła wyłącznie opóźnieniu ujawnienia wcześniejszego wprowadzenia w błąd.

**DIY dismissal risk:** proving direct intent is the hard part. The complaint should ask the prosecutor to verify objective documents that you cannot obtain yourself: bank flows, supplier invoices, customs records, courier/AWB data, mailbox access logs, and internal correspondence.

## 6. What happens after filing

After filing, the organ should register the case and issue either an opening decision or refusal. KPK art. 305 requires the preparatory-proceeding organ to issue a decision to open or refuse; it must notify the reporting person and the identified victim. ([Isap][4])

For PLN 155,000 under art. 286 § 1 KK, the case will likely be handled as **dochodzenie** rather than full **śledztwo**, because KPK art. 325b expressly includes art. 286 § 1–2 KK where the loss does not exceed 200,000 PLN. The PLN 155,000 amount is below the current “mienie znacznej wartości” threshold of more than PLN 200,000. ([Isap][4])

During art. 307 KPK screening, the authority may ask you to supplement the report or may question you as a witness. If art. 307 is used, the decision to open or refuse should be issued within **30 days** from receipt. If you receive no notice within **6 weeks**, KPK art. 306 § 3 lets you complain to the superior prosecutor or the supervisory organ. ([Isap][4])

If the prosecutor or Police refuses to open proceedings, you should receive a **postanowienie o odmowie wszczęcia dochodzenia/śledztwa** with instructions. As pokrzywdzony, you have a right to appeal the refusal under KPK art. 306 § 1 and a right to inspect the file for that appeal under art. 306 § 1b. The general KPK deadline for a complaint/appeal is **7 days from service** if the decision must be served. ([Isap][4])

Route of appeal:

**Zażalenie**
to **Sąd Rejonowy dla Krakowa-Śródmieścia w Krakowie, II Wydział Karny**
**via** Prokuratura Rejonowa Kraków-Śródmieście Wschód w Krakowie
or via the police/prosecution unit that issued or approved the refusal.

The official court page lists the II Criminal Division of the District Court for Kraków-Śródmieście at **ul. Przy Rondzie 7, 31-547 Kraków**. ([Kraków District Court][9])

If the court overturns a refusal, KPK art. 330 § 1 says the court’s indications on what must be clarified or performed are binding for the preparatory-proceeding organ. If the case is refused again after you have used the statutory appeal route twice, a subsidiary indictment may become possible, but KPK art. 55 § 2 requires such an indictment to be prepared and signed by an advocate or legal adviser. ([Isap][4])

Also include a compensation request. KK art. 46 § 1 allows the court, on the victim’s request after conviction, to order repair of the damage; KPK art. 49a allows the victim to submit that request by the close of the trial. You can file it already in the complaint. ([Isap][3])

**DIY dismissal risk:** the first refusal appeal is often where DIY becomes false economy. A good appeal must attack the “civil dispute” reasoning, point to omitted evidence, and demand specific verification steps. If the refusal relies on SN III KK 216/25, your appeal must show why your case alleges deception, not just non-performance.
