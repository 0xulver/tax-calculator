# Prompt 8 — Who runs the criminal case, my role in it, and what it costs me

## Role
You are a Polish criminal-procedure practitioner. I am the victim (pokrzywdzony) of a suspected fraud. I want to understand, concretely, who carries the burden of running the case and what I personally must do and pay — because I want to avoid paying a lawyer to "run the case" if the State does that for me.

## Case facts
- I am Magnus Brantheim, a foreign (Swedish) victim resident in Warsaw, modest means, not a Polish speaker at a legal level.
- Suspected offence: art. 286 § 1 KK fraud over a 155,000 PLN undelivered-goods transaction by Mateusz Szklarski / GPUcomputer (and possibly Waldek Łopata as co-perpetrator).
- I intend to file the zawiadomienie myself (see prompt 1) and want to know what happens procedurally afterwards and what it costs.

## What I need

1. **Who prosecutes.** Explain the **zasada legalizmu / ścigania z urzędu** (art. 10 KPK) for art. 286 KK: once a credible complaint is filed, is the **prosecutor (and police) obliged to investigate and prosecute at State expense**, gathering evidence (bank records, witness questioning, supplier verification) using powers I don't have? Confirm art. 286 is prosecuted **ex officio** (publicznoskargowe), i.e. I do not run it as a private prosecution. Note the narrow art. 286 § 4 wnioskowy caveat (prosecution on the victim's motion when the offence is committed against a close person) and confirm it doesn't apply here.

2. **My role and rights as pokrzywdzony** during the investigation:
   - rights to be informed, to submit evidence motions (wnioski dowodowe), to access files, to appeal a refusal/umorzenie (art. 306 KPK zażalenie);
   - **oskarżyciel posiłkowy** — joining as an auxiliary prosecutor once an indictment is filed (art. 53–54 KPK): what it gives me and whether it requires a lawyer;
   - the **subsidiary indictment** backstop if the prosecutor refuses twice — does *that* step require a lawyer (przymus adwokacko-radcowski)?

3. **Do I have to hire and pay a lawyer (pełnomocnik)?**
   - Is a pełnomocnik mandatory at any stage for a pokrzywdzony, or purely optional?
   - **Free court-appointed pełnomocnik z urzędu** (art. 88 + art. 78 KPK): can a victim get one, on what means-test basis, and does my foreign residence/income complicate it? How do I apply?
   - Which steps are genuinely DIY-able and which realistically need paid counsel (drafting the complaint, the subsidiary indictment, trial advocacy).

4. **What I must personally do/attend.**
   - Will I be summoned to testify, and can I give testimony by **videoconference** (art. 177 § 1a KPK) or in writing from abroad/Warsaw to avoid repeated Kraków trips?
   - Interpreter rights for a non-Polish-speaker (art. 204 KPK) — provided and paid by the State?

5. **Realistic total out-of-pocket cost** if I run it relying on the prosecutor (no private counsel): filing (0 PLN), sworn translations, travel, optional counsel — give a range, and contrast with the cost if I hire a pełnomocnik throughout.

## Output format
Lead with a plain answer to "does the prosecutor run it for free, or must I pay a lawyer?" Then the rights/role breakdown, the free-counsel eligibility, and a cost range for DIY vs. represented.
