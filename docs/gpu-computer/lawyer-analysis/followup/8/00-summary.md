# Research summary — Who runs the criminal case and what it costs me

> All five reports agree on the core answer: art. 286 § 1 KK fraud is a publicly prosecuted (ex officio) offence, so once you file a credible zawiadomienie the prosecutor and police are legally obliged (zasada legalizmu, art. 10 § 1 KPK) to investigate and prosecute at State expense — pulling bank records, compelling witnesses, verifying the supplier chain with powers you don't have. You never need a paid lawyer except for one narrow backstop: the subsidiary indictment (art. 55 § 2 KPK). Realistic DIY out-of-pocket is roughly 0–2,000 PLN; full private representation roughly 5,000–25,000 PLN.

## Key findings (consensus)

- **The State runs it.** Filing the zawiadomienie is free; the open/refuse decision is due within ~30 days. The art. 286 § 4 "close person" caveat does not apply to an arm's-length seller like Szklarski/Łopata.
- **You are a party (strona) in the investigation** (art. 299 KPK): right to be informed, file evidence motions (art. 315), access the file, and appeal a refusal/umorzenie via art. 306 zażalenie (free, DIY-able).
- **Oskarżyciel posiłkowy** (art. 53–54): after indictment, a simple written/oral declaration before the trial's przewód sądowy begins makes you a party at trial (question witnesses, file motions, appeal). No lawyer, no fee. The deadline is absolute — miss it and the status is lost.
- **Lawyer mandatory only for the subsidiary indictment** — after two refusals plus the appeal path, the indictment must be drafted and signed by an adwokat/radca prawny (przymus adwokacko-radcowski), filed within one month.
- **Free pełnomocnik z urzędu** is available to victims (art. 88 + art. 78 KPK) on a means test ("cannot pay without prejudice to necessary maintenance"); nationality/foreign income is no bar, and you can request one for a single act, e.g. drafting the subsidiary indictment.
- **Interpreter is mandatory and State-paid** for your questioning (art. 204 KPK). **Videoconference testimony** (art. 177 § 1a) can be requested to avoid Kraków trips — granted at the organ's discretion, not on demand. Written statements cannot replace oral testimony. Witness travel costs are reimbursable if claimed within 3 days (art. 618a).

## Where the models disagree or add unique points

- **Subsidiary-indictment fee:** Perplexity Computer says 300 PLN court fee; ChatGPT 5.5 Pro cites a 1,000 PLN flat expense under a regulation effective 1 July 2025. Verify if you ever reach that stage.
- **Who decides the z urzędu application during the investigation:** Gemini PDF and Perplexity say the prosecutor leading the case; ChatGPT Deep Research says the court president/referendarz. Confirm locally.
- **ChatGPT Deep Research** uniquely notes the case may run as a dochodzenie (155k < 200k PLN, art. 325b), art. 56a KPK (translated decisions served on a non-Polish-speaking auxiliary prosecutor), and the free nieodpłatna pomoc prawna network.
- **ChatGPT 5.5 Pro** provides ready-made Polish wording for the key filings and warns that a failed auxiliary-prosecutor-only appeal can shift appeal costs to you.
- **Gemini 3.1 Pro PDF** warns foreign income can look "high" against Polish living standards — document Warsaw expenses meticulously in the means declaration; it also recommends pre-translating the key contract/transfer proof before filing to avoid intake delays.
- **Perplexity (both files)** flag a doctrinal gap: the strongest interpreter guarantee (art. 72) is written for the accused, not victims — in practice it's provided, but request it explicitly. Perplexity Computer adds that a convicted defendant can be ordered to reimburse your costs.

## Action checklist

1. File the zawiadomienie yourself (0 PLN), with chronology, payment proof, correspondence, and concrete evidence motions (bank records, Łopata questioning, supplier verification).
2. In the same filing: request an interpreter (art. 204), videoconference/Warsaw questioning (art. 177 § 1a), and status updates.
3. Pre-translate only the 2–4 key documents (sworn translation, ~300–800 PLN).
4. If no decision in ~6 weeks, complain to the superior prosecutor; if refused/discontinued, file the art. 306 zażalenie yourself (consider one paid consultation, ~300–600 PLN, to sharpen it).
5. If an indictment is filed, declare oskarżyciel posiłkowy status before trial opens (free, no lawyer).
6. Claim witness travel reimbursement within 3 days of any summons; only apply for a pełnomocnik z urzędu (or pay) if you hit the subsidiary-indictment wall or a contested trial.

## Caveats / open questions

- The subsidiary-indictment cost figure (300 vs 1,000 PLN) and the correct recipient of the z urzędu application during the investigation need verification.
- Videoconference and z urzędu counsel are discretionary, not guaranteed; a weak art. 306 zażalenie is the main DIY failure risk.
- All figures are model estimates from public tariffs/price lists, not legal advice; deadline-sensitive steps (7-day zażalenie, 1-month subsidiary indictment) should be confirmed with the free legal-aid scheme or a lawyer.
