## Bottom line first: **Yes, the prosecutor runs it — and pays for it. You do not need a lawyer to prosecute this case.**

Art. 286 § 1 KK (fraud) is a *przestępstwo ścigane z urzędu* — a public-prosecution offence. Once you file a credible *zawiadomienie*, the State machinery is **legally obliged** to take over. A pełnomocnik is never mandatory for the victim at any stage of a mainstream public-prosecution fraud case. That said, there is one narrow step — the *subsydiarny akt oskarżenia* fallback — that does require a professional lawyer if you ever reach it.

## 1. Who Prosecutes — the Zasada Legalizmu

Art. 10 § 1 KPK imposes a hard statutory duty: *"Organ powołany do ścigania przestępstw jest obowiązany do wszczęcia i przeprowadzenia postępowania przygotowawczego, a oskarżyciel publiczny także do wniesienia i popierania oskarżenia — o czyn ścigany z urzędu."*  In plain terms: police and prosecutor are **compelled** to investigate once a credible complaint is filed, and the prosecutor must bring charges if evidence supports them. This is not a discretionary power — it is a legal obligation, and the State pays all investigation costs. [mfiles](https://mfiles.pl/pl/index.php/Zasada_legalizmu)

Art. 286 § 1 KK fraud is unambiguously **publicznoskargowe** (public-prosecution). The police and prosecutor gather bank records, question witnesses, verify supplier chains, and subpoena business documents using coercive powers you simply do not have as a private individual.  The narrow wnioskowy caveat in **art. 286 § 4 KK** — requiring the victim's motion before prosecution begins — applies **only when the offence was committed against a *osoba najbliższa*** (spouse, child, parent, sibling, person in cohabitation).  Mateusz Szklarski and Waldek Łopata are obviously not your close family, so § 4 does not apply and prosecution proceeds automatically ex officio upon a credible complaint. [dobryadwokat](https://dobryadwokat.pl/artykul/jak-sad-ukarze-za-oszustwo-art-286-par-1-kk,66)

## 2. Your Role and Rights as Pokrzywdzony

### During the Investigation (Postępowanie Przygotowawcze)

Under art. 299 § 1 KPK you are formally a **party** (*strona*) to the preliminary proceedings from the moment of filing.  This gives you concrete procedural rights: [trybunal.gov](https://trybunal.gov.pl/postepowanie-i-orzeczenia/komunikaty-prasowe/komunikaty-przed/art/6949-uprawnienia-pokrzywdzonego-w-procesie-karnym-pokrzywdzony-oskarzyciel-posilkowy)

- **Right to be informed** — you must receive notice of any refusal to open proceedings or of any umorzenie (discontinuation)
- **Wnioski dowodowe** — you may submit written evidence motions at any time (e.g., requesting a court order to compel GPUcomputer's bank to disclose records, requesting supplier verification in China/Taiwan)
- **Access to files** — right to inspect the *akta sprawy* before filing any appeal [sprawy-karne.biz](https://sprawy-karne.biz.pl/odmowa-wszczecia-lub-umorzenie-postepowania-zazalenie/)
- **Art. 306 KPK — zażalenie** — if the prosecutor refuses to open an investigation or discontinues it, you have **7 days** from receiving the decision to file a complaint (zażalenie) to the competent rejonowy court *via* the prosecutor who issued the decision; the complaint is **free of charge** [legaartis](https://legaartis.com/zazalenie-na-umorzenie-sledztwa-lub-dochodzenia-jak-skutecznie-zaskarzyc-decyzje-prokuratora/)

### Joining as Oskarżyciel Posiłkowy (Art. 53–54 KPK)

Once the prosecutor files an indictment with the court, you may submit a written declaration that you wish to act as **oskarżyciel posiłkowy** (auxiliary prosecutor, acting *alongside* the public prosecutor).  You must do this before the *przewód sądowy* begins at the main hearing. This status gives you the right to attend all trial sessions, submit evidence motions, cross-examine witnesses, and appeal the verdict — all while the public prosecutor continues to drive the case. **No lawyer is required to make this declaration** — a simple written statement in Polish suffices. [pwrz](http://www.pwrz.pl/komentarz_do_ustawy/kodeks_postepowania_karnego_1997/art-53-kpk.html)

### The Subsidiary Indictment Backstop (Art. 55 KPK) — ⚠️ Lawyer Required Here

If the prosecutor refuses to act and you have exhausted the art. 306 appeal route (the sąd rejonowy upheld the umorzenie, the prosecutor again declines to prosecute), you may file a *subsydiarny akt oskarżenia* — essentially becoming your own prosecutor. The preconditions are strict: you need **two refusals/umorzenia**, and the court must have upheld at least one.  At this point, **art. 55 § 2 KPK imposes mandatory *przymus adwokacko-radcowski***: the subsidiary indictment must be *drafted and signed by a professional* (adwokat, radca prawny, or radca Prokuratorii Generalnej RP).  You cannot file it yourself. The indictment also carries a **300 PLN court fee**. [adwokatslabosz](https://adwokatslabosz.eu/czym-jest-subsydiarny-akt-oskarzenia/)

In practice, for a 155,000 PLN fraud with a clear paper trail, reaching the subsidiary indictment stage is the exception — not the rule.

## 3. Do You Need to Hire a Lawyer?

### No mandatory representation for victims

At no stage of a standard *z urzędu* prosecution is a pełnomocnik mandatory for the pokrzywdzony. You can file the zawiadomienie, attend hearings, submit evidence motions, join as oskarżyciel posiłkowy, and appeal an umorzenie entirely without legal counsel. [gov](https://www.gov.pl/attachment/f4e4629e-a8f4-480d-9efe-57c8e8d4bd17)

### Free Court-Appointed Counsel — Pełnomocnik z Urzędu (Art. 88 + Art. 78 KPK)

Art. 88 KPK expressly applies art. 78 KPK to the victim's pełnomocnik, meaning a pokrzywdzony can request a court-appointed lawyer under the same means test as a defendant.  The standard is: *"wykaże, że nie jest w stanie ponieść kosztów obrony bez uszczerbku dla niezbędnego utrzymania siebie i rodziny"* — you must show you cannot pay without harming your essential subsistence. [lexlege](https://lexlege.pl/kpk/art-88/)

**How to apply:**
- During the investigation: submit a written wniosek to the **prokurator prowadzący sprawę**
- Once it reaches court: submit to the **prezes sądu** (or referendarz sądowy) of the court hearing the case [malopolska.uw.gov](https://www.malopolska.uw.gov.pl/doc/zal.11.Wniosek_przyznanie_pelnomocnika_urzedu.doc)
- The application requires a declaration of financial status, income, expenses, and family situation [malopolska.uw.gov](https://www.malopolska.uw.gov.pl/doc/zal.11.Wniosek_przyznanie_pelnomocnika_urzedu.doc)

**Does foreign residence complicate this?** No statutory provision excludes foreign victims. Polish courts assess means based on actual financial circumstances, not nationality or residence.  Living in Warsaw and working in Poland (even via JDG) is legally equivalent to being a Polish resident for this purpose. However, you should attach supporting documentation (income statements, bank statements) translated into Polish, as the court will not do this for you. [gov](https://www.gov.pl/attachment/f4e4629e-a8f4-480d-9efe-57c8e8d4bd17)

## 4. Personal Attendance — Testimony and Language

### Testimony by Videoconference

Art. 177 § 1a KPK explicitly permits testimony *"przy użyciu urządzeń technicznych umożliwiających przeprowadzenie tej czynności na odległość z jednoczesnym bezpośrednim przekazem obrazu i dźwięku"* — i.e., real-time video-and-audio link.  You can request this to avoid travel from Warsaw to Kraków. In preparatory proceedings the local prosecutor's office or police station facilitates the connection; at trial, a court official in your local (Warsaw) district court sits with you.  There is no right to purely written testimony — you must appear (in person or by video), but the video route is your legitimate tool. [standardyprawa](https://standardyprawa.pl/akt/5/art/898)

### State-Provided Interpreter

Art. 204 KPK requires the investigating authority to summon a sworn interpreter whenever a witness or party does not speak Polish adequately.  The cost of the interpreter is borne by the **State (Skarb Państwa)** as a standard procedural expense — you pay nothing for court-appointed interpretation.  This has been confirmed explicitly by the Polish National Police Command (KGP): *"osoby przesłuchiwane nie ponoszą kosztów udziału tłumacza."* [standardyprawa](https://standardyprawa.pl/akt/5/art/932)

**Important caveat:** The law is clearest about interpretation for *defendants*. For victims, one academic commentary notes the KPK does not guarantee interpretation *as a right* in quite the same terms — but in practice, prosecutors and police routinely provide it, and the RPO (Ombudsman) has pressed for consistent application.  Simply inform the investigating authority in your zawiadomienie that your Polish is insufficient for legal proceedings and request an interpreter be present at any questioning. They are required to accommodate this. [gov](https://www.gov.pl/attachment/3d3bfa42-f95f-4cca-9258-d341d1e20692)

### Key Cost-Risk Trade-Off

The main risk of going DIY is a poorly drafted art. 306 zażalenie that fails to identify specific investigative gaps — a lazy umorzenie can survive a weak appeal. A single paid consultation (2–4 hours, ~600–2,400 PLN at hourly rates of  200–600 PLN/h) to review a potential zażalenie is the most cost-effective use of paid counsel if and when the police prove reluctant. Drafting the initial zawiadomienie, joining as oskarżyciel posiłkowy, and attending hearings with a state interpreter are all genuinely within your reach without paid representation.
