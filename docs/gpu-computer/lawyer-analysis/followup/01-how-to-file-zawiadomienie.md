# Prompt 1 — How to file the fraud complaint (zawiadomienie) myself, from abroad, without a lawyer

## Role
You are a Polish criminal-procedure practitioner (prawo karne gospodarcze) writing a precise, step-by-step DIY filing manual for a foreign-resident victim who intends to file the complaint personally, without engaging paid counsel. Cite specific provisions of the Kodeks postępowania karnego (KPK) and current practice. Where rules changed recently, give the current (2025–2026) position.

## Case facts
- I am Magnus Brantheim, a Swedish citizen resident in Warsaw, Poland. In January 2026 I paid 155,000 PLN (150,000 PLN by SWIFT + 5,000 PLN, both 12.01.2026) for a custom ML workstation from GPUcomputer, citing proforma invoice 2/01-2026.
- Seller: Mateusz Szklarski (also "Mateusz Szklarski-Łopata"), sole proprietor (JDG) trading as GPUcomputer, NIP 8661681248, registered since 2015 at ul. Mogilska 16/7, Kraków (a virtual office). The proforma was issued by his firm and I paid his firm.
- A second man, Waldemar Łopata ("Waldek"), ran much of the correspondence from waldek@gpucomputer.pl and also operated the root gpucomputer@gpucomputer.pl mailbox. Shared surname Łopata; probable relatives.
- Goods never delivered. On 02.03.2026 a full refund was offered; on 10.03.2026 both mailboxes sent (one minute apart) a written promise to repay 155,000 PLN with interest within 14 business days. Nothing paid; deadline lapsed 31.03.2026.
- Contradictory explanations: 24.02.2026 "customs cleared the goods today, missing components will arrive"; 13.05.2026 "a Hong Kong supplier went bankrupt and the paid-for components never arrived."
- EPU payment-order claim already filed 08.04.2026 (Nc-e 552126/26, Sąd Rejonowy Lublin-Zachód), 155,000 PLN + statutory interest from 31.03.2026.
- KRZ shows no bankruptcy/restructuring against either man as of 28.05.2026.
- Mateusz physically lives and works full-time in the Netherlands; the Kraków business is run remotely / by Waldek.

## What I need
A practical filing manual answering, concretely:

1. **Where to file.** Which authority — Prokuratura Rejonowa vs. Policja (komisariat)? Which territorial unit is competent given (a) the seller's CEIDG address is ul. Mogilska 16/7, Kraków (a virtual office), (b) I live in Warsaw, (c) the payment originated from my account? Can I file at the prokuratura nearest to me in Warsaw and have it transferred (przekazanie według właściwości)? Give the likely competent Kraków unit and its address.

2. **How to file from abroad / remotely.** Can the zawiadomienie be filed by registered post (list polecony)? By ePUAP/e-Prokuratura — and does that require a Polish Profil Zaufany / qualified e-signature I would need to obtain? Is e-mail acceptable or procedurally fragile? Can a Polish-resident friend file it on my behalf with a power of attorney?

3. **What the document must contain** to survive the screening stage (postępowanie sprawdzające, art. 307 KPK) and not be dismissed as a mere civil dispute. Give a section-by-section skeleton: oznaczenie organu, my data as pokrzywdzony, the suspects, the precise opis czynu, the legal qualification (art. 286 § 1 KK), the uzasadnienie, the wnioski dowodowe, and the wniosek o ściganie. Show how to plead "uzasadnione podejrzenie" (reasonable suspicion) rather than asserting guilt, to avoid art. 234 KK (false accusation) exposure.

4. **The deception theory to plead.** Given Polish courts (incl. SN III KK 216/25, 17.06.2025) treat "took prepayment, didn't deliver, didn't refund" as civil breach, how should I frame the *method of deception* — e.g. concealment (przemilczenie) of supplier risk / inability to perform, and the mutually contradictory customs-vs-Hong-Kong stories — to meet the dolus directus requirement? Give concrete wording.

5. **Evidence annexes.** What to attach, and in what form (originals vs. copies). Do English-language exhibits (emails, SWIFT confirmation) need sworn Polish translations (tłumaczenie przysięgłe) at the complaint stage, or only later? Roughly how many pages and what cost.

6. **What happens after filing.** The screening/verification path; the 6-week notification rule; how I learn whether an investigation (dochodzenie/śledztwo) was opened or refused (postanowienie o odmowie wszczęcia); and the deadline and route to appeal a refusal (zażalenie, art. 306 KPK — confirm the 7-day window and the court it goes to).

7. **A ready-to-adapt Polish-language template** of the full zawiadomienie for these facts.

## Output format
A numbered manual with the Polish template at the end. Flag every point where doing it myself (vs. via a lawyer) materially raises the dismissal risk, so I can decide where DIY is false economy.
