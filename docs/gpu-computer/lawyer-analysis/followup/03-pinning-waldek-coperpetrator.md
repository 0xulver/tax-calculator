# Prompt 3 — Holding Waldek responsible: criminal co-perpetration, then civil liability

## Role
You are a Polish criminal- and civil-law specialist. The lawyer said there is no basis to pursue Waldek civilly *at this stage* because I contracted with and paid Mateusz's firm — but that a criminal investigation's findings could later open claims against Waldek. I want to understand exactly how that bridge works and what it would take to build it.

## Case facts
- I paid 155,000 PLN to Mateusz Szklarski's JDG (GPUcomputer, NIP 8661681248) on his firm's proforma invoice 2/01-2026, for an undelivered workstation.
- Waldemar Łopata ("Waldek") is the man who actually conducted most of the dealings:
  - He corresponded from **waldek@gpucomputer.pl**.
  - He also operated the **root gpucomputer@gpucomputer.pl** mailbox (on 10.03.2026 the refund promise arrived from *both* gpucomputer@ at 13:10 and waldek@ at 13:11 — near-identical text, one minute apart, showing he controls both inboxes).
  - He has co-published his own "3dkrakow" brand on gpucomputer.pl since 2016 (Wayback captures 2016/2018/2019).
  - He personally made and repeated the refund promise and gave the explanations.
  - He gave two mutually contradictory accounts: 24.02.2026 "customs cleared the goods today, the missing components will arrive"; 13.05.2026 "a Hong Kong supplier went bankrupt and the paid-for parts never arrived."
  - He is **not VAT-registered**; he shares the surname Łopata with Mateusz and is probably a relative.
- Mateusz lives and works full-time in the Netherlands (factory worker), making it physically implausible that he runs the Kraków custom-PC business day-to-day; the operational reality points to Waldek as de facto operator.
- KRZ shows no insolvency for either man (28.05.2026).

## What I need

1. **Criminal co-perpetration theory against Waldek.** Analyse, with statute and case law:
   - **art. 18 § 1 KK** (współsprawstwo / sprawstwo kierownicze) — can Waldek be a co-perpetrator or directing perpetrator of art. 286 § 1 KK fraud even though the invoice and bank account were Mateusz's? What facts establish his "panowanie nad czynem"?
   - **art. 18 § 3 KK** (pomocnictwo) and § 2 (podżeganie) as fallbacks.
   - **art. 21 § 2 KK** — how personal/individualised circumstances of a co-perpetrator are handled.
   - Does the fact that he controlled the root mailbox, made the refund promises, and gave the false/contradictory explanations support charging him as a perpetrator of the deception (wprowadzenie w błąd), independent of who issued the invoice?

2. **What evidence the prosecutor would need** to charge Waldek, and which of my existing evidence (dual-mailbox control, refund promises in his name, the contradictory stories, the 3dkrakow co-branding, his de facto operation while Mateusz is abroad) maps onto which element.

3. **The civil bridge.** Concretely, how do criminal findings against Waldek translate into civil recovery against him?
   - **art. 422 KC** (joint liability of one who caused, aided, or knowingly benefited from the damage) and **art. 441 § 1 KC** (solidarity of co-tortfeasors) — could a finding of co-perpetration make Waldek a solidary debtor for the 155k via tort (czyn niedozwolony), bypassing the contract-privity problem the lawyer raised?
   - **art. 46 KK** restitution — can a criminal court order Waldek personally to repay, if he is convicted as co-perpetrator?
   - Does a criminal conviction/finding bind a later civil court (prejudykat, art. 11 KPC)?

4. **Should the zawiadomienie name Waldek from the outset** as a suspected co-perpetrator, or name only Mateusz and let the investigation reach Waldek? Pros/cons of each.

5. **Piercing the "I only have a relationship with Mateusz's firm" objection** — any civil theory that reaches Waldek *without* a criminal conviction (e.g. tort/deceit, działanie faktyczne, firmanctwo, unjust enrichment if funds flowed to him)? Be honest about how weak/strong each is.

## Output format
Element-by-element criminal analysis, then the civil-bridge analysis, then a concrete recommendation on whether to name Waldek in the complaint now.
