# Research summary — Pinning Waldek as co-perpetrator

> All six models agree the lawyer's privity objection only kills the **contract** route: Waldek is a credible co-perpetrator of art. 286 § 1 KK fraud (a general-subject offence — whose name is on the invoice is legally irrelevant), and a conviction bridges to full personal civil liability for the 155,000 PLN via art. 46 KK restitution and tort solidarity (art. 415/422 + 441 § 1 KC). All six recommend naming Waldek in the zawiadomienie now. The pivotal evidentiary unknown is what Waldek did **before** the payment.

## Key findings (consensus)

- **Co-perpetration doctrine.** Art. 286 is a przestępstwo powszechne; under art. 18 § 1 KK and the material-objective theory (SN II KK 162/11), a co-perpetrator need not realise every statutory element — an essential contribution (istotny wkład) within a tacit agreement (porozumienie dorozumiane) suffices. Korzyść majątkowa can be "for another person" (art. 115 § 4 KK), so money landing in Mateusz's account doesn't shield Waldek. Art. 21 § 2 KK is no obstacle — fraud has no personal-status element. Pomocnictwo (art. 18 § 3, provable on dolus eventualis) is the agreed fallback; podżeganie is weakest; sprawstwo kierownicze plausible but harder.
- **Evidence mapping.** Dual-mailbox control (13:10/13:11 twin emails) → panowanie nad czynem; contradictory customs/Hong-Kong stories → knowing falsity and intent (both can't be true); 3dkrakow co-branding + Mateusz's NL employment → de facto operator and implied porozumienie. Gaps the prosecutor must close: pre-payment correspondence, mailbox/IP logs, fund flows from the JDG account, proforma authorship.
- **Timing caveat (all models).** Fraud completes at the moment of payment; post-payment refund promises are evidence of role/intent, not the actus reus itself — anchor the deception in pre-payment conduct. *(Folder 10 closes much of this gap: the pre-payment kontakt page publicly assigned Waldek the "konfiguracje" role, archived 14.11.2025.)*
- **Civil bridge.** Tort bypasses privity (art. 443 KC allows tort alongside contract). A conviction lets the criminal court order Waldek personally — solidarily — to repay under art. 46 KK (SN I KZP 40/00); art. 11 KPC then binds a civil court, **but only** as to a final conviction's operative part, and only against the person convicted — conviction of Mateusz alone does not bind against Waldek; an acquittal/umorzenie doesn't bar a civil claim. Art. 442¹ § 2 KC gives a 20-year limitation if damage flows from a crime. "Knowingly benefited" under art. 422 KC requires positive knowledge (SN III CZP 89/17).
- **Name him now** — unanimous: framed as uzasadnione podejrzenie with evidence attached, to direct the investigation, preserve volatile digital evidence, and keep the art. 46/art. 11 path open. Downsides are minimal (no suspect status attaches from a zawiadomienie alone).
