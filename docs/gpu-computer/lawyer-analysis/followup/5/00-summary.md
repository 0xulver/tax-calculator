# Research summary — EPU enforcement when the account is empty

> Once the EPU nakaz is final, the klauzula wykonalności is added electronically by the Lublin e-court; you then file a wniosek egzekucyjny immediately with a komornik of your choice (Kraków appellate area), who runs cheap electronic sweeps (OGNIVO, CEPiK, EKW, ZUS/US). With the debtor in the Netherlands and the Alior account likely empty, expect umorzenie for bezskuteczność (art. 824 § 1 pkt 3 KPC) at modest cost (~300–500 PLN total exposure). The postanowienie o umorzeniu is the real deliverable: it preserves the title for re-runs within the 6-year limitation period, supports an art. 300 KK criminal complaint, and clears the way for Dutch enforcement — which is where the money actually is.

## Key findings (consensus)

- **Procedure:** klauzula is granted ex officio in electronic form; file the wniosek the same day it appears — speed is your only domestic anti-dissipation tool. Under art. 10 u.k.k. you may pick any komornik within the Kraków appellate area (written declaration required; not for real-estate execution; busy offices may refuse).
- **Searches:** the wniosek should request all methods (real estate needs an express request), name the Alior account, and order poszukiwanie majątku (100 PLN). Komornik runs OGNIVO across all Polish banks, CEPiK, the central land-register database (searchable by PESEL), ZUS, and the tax office. Even an empty account seizure catches future inflows; a business account has no kwota wolna; tax refunds are 100% seizable.
- **JDG = no subsidiarity:** business and personal assets are one pool, attached simultaneously.
- **Costs:** zaliczki ~100–400 PLN; the 10% fee (3% on prompt voluntary payment) comes out of recovery and burdens the debtor. Bezskuteczność umorzenie costs you only 150 PLN — but withdrawal or 6 months of creditor inactivity triggers a 5% fee (~7,750 PLN). Never go silent on the komornik.
- **Bezskuteczność:** the postanowienie o umorzeniu itself is the certificate; the debt and title survive. Principal prescribes after 6 years, interest after 3; each enforcement filing interrupts the clock.
- **NL reach:** a Polish komornik has zero reach over Dutch wages or accounts; you need Brussels I bis (art. 53 certificate) or the EEO, plus a Dutch deurwaarder; EAPO can freeze Dutch bank accounts ex parte (details in folder 6).
- **Wyjawienie majątku (art. 913 KPC):** legally available but its coercive teeth (fine, forced appearance, arrest) stop at the border — value is record-building and the 5-year transaction lookback (feeds skarga pauliańska / art. 300 KK).

## Where the models disagree or add unique points

- **Timeline to umorzenie:** the Polish-language file says 1–6 months, Gemini 2–4 months, Kimi 12–24 months. Plan for "many months."
- **Kimi (docx)** is the most operationally detailed: umorzenie under pkt 3 preserves the limitation interruption, but pkt 4 (inactivity) nullifies it; re-file roughly every 3 years to keep post-judgment interest alive (calendar ~end-2029); pushes the EEO (50 zł, debtor cannot oppose recognition) over Brussels I bis — **note: folder 6 research reaches the opposite conclusion (EEO risky given EPU fiction-of-service); prefer Brussels Ia**; says the bezskuteczność document is irrelevant for Dutch enforcement — whereas both ChatGPT files call it "strategically useful" abroad.
- **Gemini** flags that the e-court may refuse/discontinue EPU if it learns the defendant's service address is foreign; ChatGPT 5.5 Pro similarly warns the debtor could attack finality on service grounds.
- **Perplexity** notes money paid directly to you (not via komornik) avoids the 10% fee; Gemini says the 3% window is 14 days vs. one month elsewhere — minor discrepancy.

## Action checklist

1. Pre-draft the wniosek egzekucyjny; file the day the klauzula appears (art. 10 declaration, Alior account, all methods, poszukiwanie majątku).
2. If any real estate surfaces, register a hipoteka przymusowa (~200 PLN) immediately.
3. Respond to every komornik communication within deadlines — avoid the 5% inactivity fee and pkt 4 umorzenie.
4. On umorzenie, keep the postanowienie + returned title; calendar a re-filing by ~3 years out.
5. Obtain the Brussels Ia art. 53 certificate early and proceed to a Dutch deurwaarder; consider EAPO for Dutch accounts.
6. Use the postanowienie plus any asset-transfer evidence for an art. 300 KK complaint.

## Caveats / open questions

- **Tax write-off correction:** several files (especially the Polish-language one, citing art. 23 ust. 1 pkt 20 PIT) claim the postanowienie enables a bad-debt deduction. This is likely **not directly applicable here**: that provision requires the amount to have been booked as przychód należny (it fits unpaid sellers, not a buyer's lost prepayment). The realistic angle is a business loss (strata), which needs accountant confirmation. Kimi's own conditions list confirms this limit.
- EPU finality is not yet obtained; service-abroad issues could derail or delay it.
- Art. 300 KK needs separate evidence of intentional asset disposition — the postanowienie proves only the effect.
