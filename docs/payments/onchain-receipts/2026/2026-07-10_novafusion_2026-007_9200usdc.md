## Tax notes

Method per topic-13 research + KIS 0114-KDIP3-1.4011.51.2025.1.AK,
0115-KDIT1.4011.25.2024.1.MR. Invoice is **EUR-denominated**, so:

- **PIT-28 (ryczałt 12%) base:** EUR face value **8.000,00 EUR** × NBP average EUR/PLN
  rate from the last business day **before the revenue date**. The USDC token count
  (9,200) / USD is NOT used for the ryczałt base — the invoice/receivable currency
  governs ("invoice face value, not the USDC market rate at transfer time"). USD
  conversion would apply only if the invoice were denominated in USD or USDC units.
- **Revenue date:** majority view = last day of billing period (Art. 14 ust. 1e) =
  **30 June 2026**, not the on-chain receipt date (minority view = receipt date).
- **No różnice kursowe** at the ryczałt layer — USDC is waluta wirtualna, not waluta obca.
- **PIT-38 (19%) cost basis:** 9,200 USDC acquired at basis = the PLN ryczałt value above;
  only later USD/PLN movement at disposal creates PIT-38 gain/loss (absorbed by cost pool).
- ⚠️ If the *contract* denominates the fee in USD (not EUR), use NBP USD/PLN on the USD amount instead.
- Policy reference: `docs/tax-law/13-jdg-stablecoin-payments/synthesis-jdg-stablecoin-payments.md`
